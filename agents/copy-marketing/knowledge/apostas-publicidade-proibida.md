# Fixed-odds betting: status as of October 2, 2026 (Brazil)

This situation changes fast and is being contested. **Confirm the rule in force and its progress in Congress before any piece.** This knowledge base is valid until November 24, 2026 and must be reviewed before then. This note covers Brazilian law only.

## What MP 1.394/2026 does

**Provisional Measure (Medida Provisória) no. 1.394 of September 25, 2026** bans, across Brazilian territory, the **operation, offering, intermediation and advertising** of fixed-odds betting lotteries, in physical or online form, including by an operator based abroad. It covers sports betting and online casinos.

- In force since its publication. Published timeline: balance withdrawals from September 25 to October 5, 2026; websites and apps offline from October 6, 2026.
- Non-compliance is subject to a warning, suspension and a fine; the amounts are in the text of the MP.
- Congress must convert it into law within 60 days, extendable by another 60. There are lawsuits before the Supreme Court (STF) challenging the measure. Its status may change: confirm on the Brazilian Congress website.

## How the solver behaves

While the MP is in force, **do not write ads, affiliate posts or promotional content for fixed-odds betting**, even for an operator that used to be licensed.

1. Explain the reason, citing MP no. 1.394/2026 and Law no. 14.790/2023.
2. Offer lawful alternatives: closure notices, guidance on withdrawals and returning balances to customers, with no call to bet.
3. Official lotteries and other types of games have their own rules: confirm before writing.
4. If the MP lapses or is suspended, the earlier rules (below) apply again; treat that as fact only after the user brings the new rule.

## Rules before the MP (context)

- **Law 14.790/2023** regulated fixed-odds betting. Arts. 16 and 17 prohibit, in advertising, unfounded claims about winnings, presenting betting as socially attractive or a sign of success (including through celebrities), suggesting it as an alternative to a job, a financial solution, extra income or an investment, and targeting minors.
- **Portaria SPA/MF 1.231/2024**: banned immediate calls to action and advertising where minors are the main audience, required warning clauses of at least 10% of the ad and made the operator liable for abusive advertising by contracted influencers.
- **Law 15.358/2026** (March 24, 2026): banned advertising by **unlicensed** operators, including links, promo codes and affiliate links leading to them.
