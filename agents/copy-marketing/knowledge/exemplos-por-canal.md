# Ready-made examples by channel

Fictional businesses, only to show format and counting. Counts checked on October 2, 2026. Never copy numbers, deadlines or conditions from the examples: they come from the user's briefing. The businesses and prices are Brazilian (R$ = Brazilian reais).

## Meta (Instagram and Facebook), PAS structure

| Field | Text (count) | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Primary text | Cake that sinks in the middle? Our jar cakes leave the oven daily at 7 am. Order by 4 pm, get it tomorrow in Pinheiros. (119) | hook and condition within the ~125 visible |
| Headline | Jar cake delivered tomorrow (27) | up to ~40 |
| Description | Order by 4 pm (13) | up to ~30, no essential information |

Why it works: a concrete problem, a solution with a verifiable condition (cutoff time and neighborhood), a single CTA.

## Email: subject and preheader

| Field | Text (count) |
|---|---|
| Subject | Your cart is still here (23) |
| Preheader | Free shipping over R$ 150 until Sunday (38) |
| Sender | Ana, from Store X |

The preheader complements the subject; it does not repeat it. Footer: company name, reason for receiving and a visible unsubscribe link.

## SMS: two ways out

SMS accents are the point of this example, so the two messages below are kept in Portuguese, the language of the Brazilian audience, with an English gloss.

| Version | Text (count) | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| No accents (GSM-7) | Pizzaria Bella: toda terca, pizza grande com 20% off. Peca no WhatsApp (11) 90000-0000. Resp SAIR p/ nao receber (112) | 160 |
| With accents (Unicode) | Bella: pizza grande -20% toda terça. SAIR p/ parar (50) | 70 |

Gloss: "Bella Pizzeria: every Tuesday, large pizza 20% off. Order on WhatsApp. Reply SAIR (STOP) to opt out."

A single "ç" or "ã" drops the limit from 160 to 70. Tell the user which version you used and why. English text has no accent problem, but an emoji, a curly quote or any other non-GSM-7 character triggers the same drop.

Only for contacts who opted in. The template needs Meta's approval in the marketing category.

## Influencer paid-post caption

> #publi I tested Brand X's vitamin C serum for 30 days. With coupon BIA10 you get 10% off until Sunday. (102)

- Disclosure on the first line, within the ~125 visible characters; also turn on the platform's "Paid partnership" label.
- An account of use and the coupon condition, without "my spots disappeared".
- Before publishing, the influencer confirms with the brand the coupon's validity and what the brand can prove.
