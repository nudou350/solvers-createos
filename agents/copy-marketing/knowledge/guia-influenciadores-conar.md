# CONAR guide for digital influencers (Brazil)

CONAR is Brazil's advertising self-regulation council. Knowledge checked on October 2, 2026 against legal analyses of the May 2026 edition, effective from June 2026 (sources disagree on the exact publication day: check the date and the text on the CONAR website). It guides the writing; it does not replace the guide or legal advice.

## When it is advertising

It is advertising when a brand, product or service is promoted **and** there is a mutual commitment with the advertiser, directly or through an agency: payment, commission, barter, an ambassador contract, a partnership or affiliation.

- It also applies to the influencer's **own brand**.
- Affiliates are expressly covered: promotion paid per clicks, sales, sign-ups or other measurable results, including trackable links and coupons (a commission coupon is an advertising relationship).
- Advertising content **generated or edited by AI** follows the same rules of the Code and the Guide.
- CDC art. 36 (Brazil's Consumer Defense Code) still applies on top of the Guide.

## How to disclose

The disclosure must be **clear, prominent and immediate**, visible **in the foreground**: it cannot depend on a click on "see more", scrolling the caption or opening the bio.

- Prioritize the platform's **native tool** (the "Paid partnership" label or equivalent).
- When there is none, use clear expressions such as **#publi** or **#publicidade**, at the start of the text.
- In video, the disclosure goes in speech and on screen from the beginning, not only at the end.
- For affiliates, disclose the relationship just as immediately; use the platform's wording or a clear sentence ("affiliate link, I earn a commission").

## Product received without payment

The Guide treats as an **activated message** a mention motivated by a non-monetary benefit, such as a gift, an invitation, a trip or accommodation, with no commitment to post.

- The relationship that led to the mention must be disclosed whenever it can influence the content or the credibility of the message.
- When in doubt between "received" and "hired", treat it as advertising and disclose: it is the safest option.
- Safe wording: "I received this product as a gift from brand X and tested it for 30 days."
- The Guide does not set specific hashtags for this case: do not invent an "official hashtag".

## Children and teenagers

- They require parental consent, an appropriate setting and legal compliance; **prior court authorization** for the use of minors' image in advertising may be needed.
- Do not ask a child to convince an adult to buy (CDC art. 37, §2; CONAR Code, art. 37).
- When a minor appears in the content, refer the user to a lawyer before publishing.

## Writing rule for a paid-post caption

1. The disclosure comes on the **first line**, before the hook, and counts within the ~125 characters visible in the feed.
2. Also turn on the platform's native label, when there is one.
3. An honest account of use ("I use it in the morning, before sunscreen"), with no result the brand cannot prove.
4. Coupon and offer condition (validity, percentage) in the caption, before the "see more".
5. No therapeutic claim for a cosmetic or supplement (see the Anvisa knowledge base).
