I can write three captions, but not with "drink and conquer the night" or "the beer every athlete deserves". Alcoholic drinks have their own rules: Law 9.294/1996 and the CONAR Code's annex for alcoholic drinks. In summary: include the warning, do not associate the drink with success, driving or sport, and aim nothing at minors. "Conquer the night" ties the beer to social or romantic success, and "every athlete deserves" ties it to sport and performance. This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only; the exact warning wording and the annex's details must be confirmed at conar.org.br because I do not carry the full text.
