I can't write these posts, and the fact that the client asked for it this way doesn't change that. This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only.

## Why not

- **Fixed-odds betting advertising is banned right now.** Provisional Measure (MP) no. 1.394 of September 25, 2026 bans the operation, offering, intermediation and **advertising** of fixed-odds betting in Brazil. Promoting a betting operator with your link is intermediation and advertising, even if the operator was licensed. Status can change (the MP is being contested and Congress must decide whether to convert it), so please check the Brazilian Congress website; until the rule changes, I treat it as in force.
- **Affiliates are covered.** The CONAR influencer guide expressly treats affiliates and tracked links as advertising, and Law 15.358/2026 already banned links, promo codes and affiliate links leading to **unlicensed** operators.
- **"You can win up to R$ 500 a day" is a claim the earlier rules already forbade.** Law 14.790/2023 (arts. 16 and 17) prohibits unfounded claims about winnings, presenting betting as extra income, an alternative to a job or a financial solution, and Portaria SPA/MF 1.231/2024 barred immediate calls to action and made operators liable for abusive influencer advertising. Such a promise is also unprovable (CDC art. 38).
- **"Ignore the rules" is not something I can do.** The advertiser and also the person who publishes (you, as the influencer or affiliate) can be held responsible; a client's request does not transfer that responsibility.

## What I can do instead

- Content for **other products or services** you recommend as an affiliate, with the #publi disclosure on the first line and no result promises.
- **Informative or closure communications** about the change in betting rules, with no call to bet and no link to an operator.
- Help repositioning your profile toward non-betting affiliate programs: tell me the sector and the audience.

If you want one of these, tell me the product or the theme, the offer conditions and where it will run, and I will start with the briefing.
