I can write the headline and the CTA, but not with those two phrases. This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only.

- **"Last spots!"** on a course that is open all year is false urgency, which is misleading advertising (CDC art. 37). Urgency and scarcity are only allowed when true and verifiable (a real date or a real number of seats).
- **"Results guaranteed or your money back"** mixes two things. "Results guaranteed" is a promise of results you cannot prove (CDC art. 38 puts the burden of proof on whoever advertises). A **refund policy** is different: you can state it if it is real, with its exact conditions (period, how to request, what is covered), and the offer binds you (CDC art. 30). Whether any legal withdrawal right also applies to online sales is not covered in my knowledge base, so confirm it at the official source or with a lawyer.
