## Rules that shape the caption

- **Disclosure on the first line**, before the hook, visible without tapping "see more" (it counts within the ~125 characters shown in the feed). Use **#publi** or **#publicidade**, and also turn on Instagram's native "Paid partnership" label. If it is a Reel, say it out loud and show it on screen from the start, not only at the end.
- **No therapeutic claim.** A moisturizer is a cosmetic: it cannot "treat", "cure", "eliminate" or "regenerate" (Anvisa RDC 752/2022). Any benefit such as "hydrates for 24 hours" needs proof of efficacy kept by the brand (CDC art. 38).
- **An honest account of use** instead of a result ("in 7 days my spots disappeared"). Only write what you actually experienced.
- **No false urgency** ("before it runs out") unless the brand confirms real limited stock (CDC art. 37).

## Briefing (draft to confirm)

- **Sector:** cosmetic, influencer paid post. Identification: #publi on the first line plus the native label.
- **Limit:** ~125 visible characters before "see more" (organic caption up to 2,200).
- **Assumptions (please correct):** you have already used the moisturizer for a while, your tone is relaxed, and there is no coupon yet.

## Questions

1. Brand and product name? How long have you used it, and how (morning, night, which step)?
2. What did you actually notice (texture, feel, scent)? Only that goes in.
3. What does the brand say it can prove about the product?
4. Is there a coupon, a link or an offer? Its validity and percentage must be in the caption.
5. Any words or tones you avoid? Is it a feed post or a Reel?
