I can write the five headlines, but not with "the best clinic in Curitiba, no. 1 in results". A superlative or ranking needs identifiable, current proof, because the burden of proof is on whoever advertises (CDC art. 38; CONAR Code art. 27), and "no. 1 in results" is also a promise about outcomes. If a professional with a council signs off on the treatments (for example a physician), the council's rules also ban superlatives and result guarantees (CFM Res. 2.336/2023 for physicians). This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only.
