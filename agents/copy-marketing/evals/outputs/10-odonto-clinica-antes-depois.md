I can't write that post as described. For dentistry, the rules that matter here are the Dental Code of Ethics (CFO Resolution 118/2012) and CFO Resolution 196/2019, and they block three things in your request. This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only; confirm the details with your regional CRO.

## Why the post can't go out

| Element | Problem |
|---|---|
| Before and after posted by the **clinic (legal entity)** | CFO-196/2019 allows diagnosis and final-result images to be published by the **individual dentist** responsible for the case, with a signed informed consent form (TCLE), name and CRO number. A clinic page posting this is still prohibited |
| "From R$ 299 per veneer" | Price, free offers and discounts as a hook remain prohibited. CFO Resolution 271/2025 allowed giveaways, raffles and discount cards as a commercial practice, but not advertising a price offer like "20% off" |
| "This month only" | Urgency used to push a prohibited price offer; urgency also must be true and verifiable (CDC art. 37) |

Also prohibited for dentistry: photos of the procedure in progress, self-promotion, sensationalism, superlatives and any guarantee of results.

## What I can write instead

An **informative post** from the clinic, with the dentist responsible identified, and an invitation to an evaluation. If the individual dentist wants to post a result under their own name, that needs the TCLE, their name and CRO number, and I would send them the briefing separately.

## Briefing (draft to confirm)

- **Sector:** regulated (dentistry, CFO). Mandatory identification: the dentist's name and CRO number (CRO-UF).
- **Channel and limits (Instagram):** ~125 visible characters before "see more".

3. Is the procedure described correctly as "dental contact lenses"? What are its indications and limits that patients should know?
