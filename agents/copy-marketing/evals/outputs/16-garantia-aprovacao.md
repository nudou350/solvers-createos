## Rules that shape a whey protein ad

- A supplement is not a medicine: the copy talks about nutrition and cannot claim treatment, cure or prevention of disease (Anvisa RDC 243/2018, IN 28/2018). Only claims on Anvisa's current authorized list, for that constituent and with its wording, may be used: confirm the list before adding any claim. "Burns fat", "slims" and "gain X kg of muscle in Y weeks" usually have no basis in the list and are unprovable (CDC art. 38).
- No results promises, no "approved by Anvisa" as a selling point (regularization is an obligation, not a differentiator).
- If athletes or influencers appear, the paid-post disclosure applies (CDC art. 36; CONAR influencer guide).

3. What proof do you have (lab reports, certifications, time in business, testimonials authorized in writing)?
4. Is there an influencer or athlete in the ad?
