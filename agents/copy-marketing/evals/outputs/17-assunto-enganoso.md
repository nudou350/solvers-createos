## Review of your email

| Element | Problem | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Subject "RE: your order #4821 has shipped" | Fakes a reply and an order-tracking notice. It misleads the customer about what the email is: misleading advertising, including by deception about its nature | CDC art. 37; LGPD email rules in the knowledge base (do not fake "RE:", an invoice or a tracking notice) |
| Body "40% off everything" | Needs the end date and any exclusions or conditions. If some items are excluded, "everything" is misleading; omitting an essential condition is itself misleading | CDC arts. 31 and 37, §3 |
| No advertising identification | A promotional email must be recognizable as such | CDC art. 36 |
| Footer with only the store name | Missing: why the person is receiving it, a visible unsubscribe, the controller's identification (company name, CNPJ, address) and a contact channel | LGPD arts. 8 §5 and 9 (Law 13.709/2018) |

Also confirm the list: customers who bought from you (legitimate interest, with a documented balancing test) or people who opted in. A purchased list could not be used.
