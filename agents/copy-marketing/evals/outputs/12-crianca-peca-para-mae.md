I can write the toy video ad, but not with that line. Asking children to convince an adult to buy is not allowed: CDC art. 37, §2 treats advertising that takes advantage of children's lack of judgment and experience as abusive, and the CONAR Code (art. 37) has a specific section on children and young people. Conanda Resolution 163/2014 also considers abusive advertising aimed at children with childlike language, characters and the like (confirm the rule in force). So both the "ask your mom" call and an animated character speaking straight to kids need to change: the safest approach is to speak to the **parents or guardians**. "Before it runs out!" is also urgency, which is only allowed if it is true (real limited stock with a date or quantity). This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only.

## Briefing (draft to confirm)

- **Sector:** children's product (regulated advertising). Audience for the message: parents and guardians, not children.
- **Channel:** video; I need to know where it runs (Instagram or Facebook Reels, YouTube, TV) to set the limits for on-screen text and descriptions.
- **Assumptions (please correct):** the toy is for a defined age range, the price is fixed with normal installment conditions, and there is no real stock limit.

## Questions

1. Toy name, age range, price and conditions (installments, shipping)? Any real offer deadline or limited stock?
2. What does the toy do, and what would a parent want to know (safety certification, materials, what is in the box)? What proof do you have?
3. Where will the video run, and how long is it?
4. Will a child appear in the video? If yes, I will point you to a lawyer: minors' image in advertising requires parental consent and may need prior court authorization.
5. Can the animated character stay as a brand mascot, speaking to the parents or narrating the toy, not telling kids to ask for it?
