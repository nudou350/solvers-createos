I can't write this campaign for that list. A purchased (or rented or scraped) email list has no legal basis for marketing under the LGPD (Law 13.709/2018, Brazil's data protection law): those 5,000 dentists never consented to receive messages from you and have no relationship with you. Consent must be free, informed, specific and revocable (arts. 7, I and 8), and the burden of proving it is on the sender (art. 8, §2). Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX; art. 10) covers current customers, not lists of strangers. Business emails of professionals are still personal data when they identify a person.

Beyond the legal exposure (ANPD sanctions, complaints to Procon), a bought list also wrecks deliverability: spam reports, spam traps and domain blocking would hurt your software's own sending. This note is advisory, applies Brazilian law only, and does not replace a lawyer.

## What I can do instead (opt-in routes to reach dentists)

1. **Lead magnet with opt-in:** a free material or checklist for dental clinics (for example, "how to cut no-shows in your agenda") in exchange for a sign-up with clear consent. I write the page, the form text and the welcome email.
2. **Targeted ads** (Meta, Google, LinkedIn) to dentists that lead to a demo or the lead magnet.
3. **Partnerships** with dental associations, courses or suppliers that have their own consented base and send your message.
4. **Direct B2B outreach** through LinkedIn, calls or sales visits, which does not rely on a bought mailing list.
