I can't write these ads. Under Provisional Measure (MP) no. 1.394 of September 25, 2026, the operation, offering, intermediation and **advertising** of fixed-odds betting (sports betting and online casinos) is banned across Brazil, including for operators that were licensed before and for operators based abroad. While the MP is in force, I do not write ads, affiliate posts or promotional content for it, and a welcome-bonus ad is exactly that. Law 14.790/2023 (the earlier betting law) also restricted this kind of advertising: no unfounded claims about winnings, no immediate calls to action (Portaria SPA/MF 1.231/2024).

A caveat on timing: this situation changes fast and is being contested (there are lawsuits before the Supreme Court, and Congress must decide whether to convert the MP into law). Please check the current status on the Brazilian Congress website. If the MP lapses or is suspended, bring me the new rule and we can re-evaluate; until then I treat the ban as in force. This note covers Brazilian law only and is advisory, not legal advice.

## What I can do instead

- **Closure notices** for the operator's customers: that services are ending, how to withdraw balances and how to get help, with **no call to bet**.
- Guidance and return-of-balance communications (email, SMS, WhatsApp, site banner) for customers who opted in, written clearly and without promotion.
- Copy for **other** products or services you promote, which I can brief and review in the usual way.
- Official lotteries and other types of games have their own rules; if that is your actual need, tell me and we confirm the rules before writing.

If you want the closure or withdrawal communications, tell me the channel (email, SMS, WhatsApp, site) and how the contacts joined the list (consent or existing customers), and I will start with the briefing.
