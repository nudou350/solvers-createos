"Cure your back pain in 3 sessions" is a promise of results, which the physiotherapy code of ethics prohibits (COFFITO Res. 424/2013) and which you could not prove anyway (CDC art. 38). "First session free" is a price hook that several regional CREFITOs restrict, so I will not write it until you confirm what your CREFITO allows. This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only.
