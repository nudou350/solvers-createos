I can write the three Instagram ads, but not with "helps cure arthrosis" or "lose weight". A food supplement is not a medicine, and its copy cannot claim treatment, cure or prevention of disease, nor any claim outside Anvisa's list of authorized claims (RDC 243/2018 and IN 28/2018). Terms like "slims" or "burns fat" usually have no basis in that list either. These claims also fail CDC art. 38 (the advertiser must prove them) and can mislead consumers with a health condition (CDC art. 37). So the pieces go out without them. This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only.
