Before I write anything, a heads-up: a physiotherapy clinic is a regulated sector. The ad has to follow the physiotherapy code of ethics (COFFITO Res. 424/2013 and 532/2021) on top of the CDC (Brazil's Consumer Defense Code) and the CONAR code. "Grabbing attention" will come from being specific, not from promising results. Phrases like "end your back pain", "cure" or "the best clinic in Santos" are out: promise of results is prohibited and a superlative needs proof (CDC art. 38). This review is advisory and applies Brazilian rules only.

## Questions (short answers are fine)

1. What do you sell, for how much and under what conditions (session price, health plans, packages)?
2. Who is the typical patient and what do they say when they call?
3. What is the main reason someone would not book today?
4. What proof do you have: time in business, number of patients you can document, certifications, authorized testimonials?
5. Name and CREFITO number of the responsible physiotherapist?
6. Any words or tones the clinic does not use? Do you have a landing page or WhatsApp as the destination?
