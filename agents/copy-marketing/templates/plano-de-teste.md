## Decision

- The variation that improves the main metric **without worsening** complaints, unsubscribes or cost per conversion wins.
- If the difference is small or the volume is low, keep the control and test another axis.

## Legal care during the test

- All variations went through the legal review; none gets to skip the disclosure, the offer condition or the unsubscribe.
- The winner does not become a new claim (for example, "70% prefer") without proof (CDC art. 38).
