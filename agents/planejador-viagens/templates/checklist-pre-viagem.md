# Pre-trip checklist: {{destination}}

**Departure:** {{departure_date}} · **Return:** {{return_date}}

> Convert each relative deadline into a real date from the departure date. Remove the items that do not apply and add the ones specific to the destination.

## 6 months or more before ({{date}})
- [ ] Check each traveler's passport validity (6 months for most destinations; Schengen: 3 months after departure and issued less than 10 years ago)
- [ ] Start the visa process, if needed (USA: check the wait time on travel.state.gov; Brazilians need a B1/B2 visa, ESTA does not apply)
- [ ] Set the budget and start buying currency gradually

## 3 to 6 months before ({{date}})
- [ ] Book or renew the passport with the Federal Police (gov.br), if needed
- [ ] Buy flights (check the baggage allowance of the fare)
- [ ] Book lodging (prefer free cancellation)
- [ ] Check the yellow fever vaccine requirement and the CIVP (Anvisa)
- [ ] Electronic authorization, depending on the destination and the travel date (UK ETA, Canada eTA, Mexico SAE, ETIAS if already in force): confirm on the official site

## 1 to 3 months before ({{date}})
- [ ] Buy tickets for attractions with quotas and high-speed trains
- [ ] Buy travel insurance (minimum medical cover for the destination; €30,000 for Schengen)
- [ ] Minor travel authorization, if applicable: form with a notarized signature at a notary's office or AEV through e-Notariado (a gov.br signature is not valid); carry 2 copies. Confirm on gov.br
- [ ] Doctor or travel medicine appointment, if there is a health condition or a vaccine
- [ ] International Driving Permit (Detran, the Brazilian state traffic authority), if you will rent a car

## 2 to 4 weeks before ({{date}})
- [ ] Reconfirm entry rules on the official site (Itamaraty Consular Portal, the destination's consulate)
- [ ] Confirm the current IOF rate (Brazil's tax on spending abroad) and the payment method (global account, card, cash)
- [ ] Get an eSIM or SIM card and download offline maps
- [ ] Set aside regular medications + a margin and prescriptions (active ingredient, in English)
- [ ] Buy a plug adapter

## Last week ({{date}})
- [ ] Online check-in
- [ ] Save digital copies of passport, visa, insurance and bookings (cloud + email)
- [ ] Write down on paper: the insurer's, the bank's and the Brazilian consulate's phone numbers at the destination
- [ ] Check the weather forecast and adjust the luggage
- [ ] Weigh the luggage

## Departure day ({{departure_date}})
- [ ] Documents, medications, charger and a change of clothes in carry-on baggage
- [ ] Arrive at the airport 3h early (international) or 1h30 to 2h early (domestic)
- [ ] Cash above US$ 10,000 (or the equivalent): declare to the Receita Federal (Brazilian tax authority) through the e-DBV
