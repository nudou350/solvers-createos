**Why**
- For Italy (Schengen area, the group of European countries that share one border system), the passport must be valid for at least **3 months after the date you leave the area**, counted from your **departure**, not from arrival.
- You leave on June 5, 2027, so the passport would need to be valid until at least **September 5, 2027**. Yours expires in August 2027: it falls short by a few weeks.
- Also, airlines check this first, at boarding, before Italian immigration even sees you. Many airlines and countries also apply a "6 months of validity" rule of thumb; yours would not meet that either.

**What I recommend**
1. **Renew the passport before buying the tickets.** You have plenty of time (about 7 months). **The rest of the entry checklist for Italy (17 days, well within the 90 days in any 180)**
- No visa for tourism (exemption of up to 90 days in any 180-day period).
- **EES**: biometric registration (photo and fingerprints) at the border on your first entry, with no prior application and no fee. Expect queues; it varies by airport.
- **ETIAS**: a prior electronic authorization (expected €20, valid 3 years). As of October 2, 2026 the European Commission indicated a start in the last quarter of 2026 with no exact date, and it has been postponed before. Please check **travel-europe.europa.eu** before you travel, and avoid intermediary sites that charge to "issue" it.
- Travel insurance with at least **€30,000** in medical cover. Buy it after the flight, and carry a printed policy.
