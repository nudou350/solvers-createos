Good news, with one thing to check today: **for tourism of up to 90 days, Brazilians don't need a visa for Japan**. The exemption was extended from September 30, 2026 to **September 29, 2029**, so April 2027 is covered.

**But it only applies to an ordinary passport with an electronic chip.** Your blue ordinary passport qualifies as the type, but you must confirm it has the chip: look for the small chip symbol on the cover. A passport **without a chip requires a visa**. If yours doesn't have it, start the renewal early (Federal Police appointment 60 to 90 days ahead) or the visa process with the consulate.
