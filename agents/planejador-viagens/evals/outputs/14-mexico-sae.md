Yes, Mexico requires a visa from Brazilians, **but since February 2026 there is an easier route for your case**: tourists who **arrive by air** (you are flying to Cancún) can request the **electronic authorization (SAE)** online, with no trip to the consulate. It is valid for **one entry**, and it is for tourism or business with no paid activity.

**Your document list for Cancún**
1. **Passport** valid for the trip (as a rule of thumb, at least 6 months on the return date). Each traveler needs their own, children included.
2. **SAE (electronic authorization)**: one per traveler, requested online before you fly. Don't leave it for the last week. Confirm the requirements, the fee and the processing time on **gob.mx/sre** or with the Mexican consulate; I won't quote an amount as certain.
3. **Exemptions:** if you hold a valid visa or permanent residence in the USA, Canada, the United Kingdom, Japan or the Schengen area, you don't need the Mexican visa. If you arrive by land or sea, you would need the visa at the consulate (not your case).
4. **Minors:** if a child travels without both parents, a travel authorization from the absent parent is required (notarized signature or AEV through e-Notariado; a gov.br signature is not valid).
5. **Travel insurance:** buy after the flight, with solid medical cover and 24h support in Portuguese. Emergency number in Mexico: 911.
6. A return ticket and proof of lodging are good to have printed; at the border they may ask.

**February in Cancún**
- It is the dry season (December to April), which is the best weather and also the more expensive time. Hurricane season (June to November) and the sargassum on the beaches (April to September) are not a concern in February.
- Carnival falls in February or March depending on the year, and Brazilian short international trips get more expensive then: check the date before booking.

**8 days:** very comfortable for one base in Cancún or the Riviera Maya, with a couple of day trips and a free day. If you prefer two bases, keep at least 3 nights in each.

**To confirm on an official site:** SAE requirements, fee and validity (gob.mx/sre), passport (gov.br/pf), IOF rate (Receita Federal or your bank).
