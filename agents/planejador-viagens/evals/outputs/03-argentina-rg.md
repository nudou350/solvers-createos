**No, a driver's license (CNH) is not accepted** to enter Argentina, or for any other international trip, as an identity document.

**What works for Argentina**
Brazilians can enter as tourists in Argentina (and Uruguay, Paraguay, Chile, Bolivia, Peru, Colombia, Ecuador and Venezuela) with one of these, always the **physical** document:
- a **passport**; or
- a state **RG** (Brazilian identity card); or
- the new **National Identity Card (CIN)**.

**What is not accepted:** the CNH, professional council cards (OAB, CRM and similar), the work card, an adult's birth certificate, and the **digital** RG or CIN in the gov.br app.

**Check your RG carefully**
- It must be in good condition, with a recognizable photo, and issued **at most 10 years ago**. This is what airlines and immigration in the region apply. An older RG is often turned away at boarding, even if it is still valid in Brazil.
- If your RG is older than 10 years (or damaged), you have two options: renew it, or issue a passport. For a passport, start at least 60 days before the trip (90 in busy seasons) on gov.br with the Federal Police.

**Other points**
- No visa is needed for tourism.
- Travel insurance is not always checked, but I recommend one with about US$ 30,000 in medical cover.
- If a child or teenager is traveling and not with both parents, a travel authorization is required (a gov.br signature is not valid). Tell me if that applies.
- Money: in Argentina the cheapest way to pay changes often, so research close to the date and check the current IOF rate with the Receita Federal or your bank.

**To confirm on an official site:** the current list of accepted documents on the Itamaraty Consular Portal (gov.br/mre) and the RG/passport rules at gov.br/pf.
