Short answer: **for Paris and Rome in December 2026 you don't need a visa, and as of October 2, 2026 there is no ETIAS to apply for yet.** But there are a few things to prepare. Here is the documents list for you and your mother.

**1. Passports (each of you)**
- Valid for at least **3 months after the date you leave the Schengen area** (the group of European countries sharing one border system) and **issued less than 10 years ago**. Count from your *departure*, not arrival. Many airlines also use a 6-month rule of thumb.
- December is close: if either passport needs renewal, the Federal Police appointment queue is the risk. Start at least 60 days ahead (90 in busy seasons). Tell me the month/year of each passport's expiry and I'll check it.

**2. Visa:** none for tourism. Brazilians have visa exemption for up to **90 days in any 180-day period**; 12 days is well within that.

**3. ETIAS (the authorization you are asking about)**
- It is a prior online authorization for travelers who don't need a visa, Brazilians included. The European Commission indicates a start in the **last quarter of 2026**, with the exact date announced months in advance. It has been postponed several times, and as of October 2, 2026 **no exact date had been announced**.
- When it starts: **€20 per person**, valid up to 3 years (or until the passport expires), requested online before boarding. The start will be phased: about 6 months of transition when nobody is turned away for lacking it, followed by about 6 months of tolerance for first-time entrants.
- So I can't tell you it is mandatory in December, and I can't tell you it will not be. **Check travel-europe.europa.eu right before you buy your tickets and again 2 to 4 weeks before leaving.** The only official site is that one; be wary of sites that charge extra to "issue" it.

**4. EES (the border registration)**
- It records your photo and fingerprints at the border when you enter the Schengen area without a visa and replaces the passport stamp. There is **no prior application and no fee**. It started on October 12, 2025 and has been in full operation since April 10, 2026.
- In September 2026 the press reported that several countries, among them France and Italy, were applying biometric collection flexibly, with no new deadline. In practice: on your first entry expect queues and some extra time, and it varies by airport. Confirm on travel-europe.europa.eu.

**5. Insurance:** medical cover of at least **€30,000** per person. The formal requirement is for visa applicants, but treat it as mandatory and carry a printed policy. For your mother, check the age table, pre-existing conditions and 24h support in Portuguese, since some plans reduce cover or raise the price above 70 or 75.

**6. December in Paris and Rome**
- Low season with Christmas markets and short days (it gets dark before 5 pm). Prices spike around Christmas and New Year.
- 12 days, 2 cities: I'd suggest about 6 nights in each, with day trips (Versailles from Paris), a free afternoon on major-attraction days and fewer stairs for your mother. Paris to Rome is a long way by train, so a flight is usually better: check options and timetables on the operators' sites.
