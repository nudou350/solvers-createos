- [ ] Check each traveler's passport: for Portugal (Schengen area) it must be valid for **3 months after your departure from Europe** and issued less than 10 years ago
- [ ] If a passport needs renewal: book the Federal Police appointment on gov.br (start by **February 9**, 90 days before; **March 11** is the latest at 60 days)
- [ ] Set the budget and start buying currency gradually

## November 10, 2026 to February 10, 2027 (3 to 6 months before)
- [ ] Buy flights (check the baggage allowance of the fare you buy)
- [ ] Book lodging, preferably with free cancellation. May is shoulder season, so book early
- [ ] Check ETIAS status (see the block below)

## February 10 to April 10 (1 to 3 months before)
- [ ] Buy tickets for quota attractions and trains, as soon as sales open

- [ ] Doctor or travel-medicine appointment, only if you have a health condition. No yellow fever vaccine is needed for this route as far as I know, but confirm with Anvisa

## To confirm on an official site
- [ ] Entry rules: Itamaraty Consular Portal (gov.br/mre) and the Portuguese consulate
- [ ] **EES** (photo and fingerprints at the border on your first entry, no prior application) and **ETIAS** (expected in the last quarter of 2026, no exact date as of October 2, 2026): travel-europe.europa.eu
- [ ] Current IOF rate: Receita Federal or your bank
- [ ] Shopping allowance when you return: Receita Federal Traveler's Guide

- [ ] Opening hours of attractions you will book

## Contingency plan
- **Lost passport:** police report and contact the Brazilian consulate
- **Health problem:** call the insurer's hotline first (saved on the phone and on paper); in a life-threatening situation, go to the emergency room and notify them afterwards. Emergency number in Portugal: 112
- **Lost card:** have the bank's phone number written down outside your phone, and carry the backup card separately
- **Cancelled flight:** keep all receipts; rights vary by country and airline
