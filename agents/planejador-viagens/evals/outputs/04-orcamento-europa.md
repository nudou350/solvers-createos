- Lodging of R$ 5,600 is about R$ 467 per night: workable for a simple hotel or apartment, but tight in Lisbon in high season.

**Hidden costs to keep in mind (put on separate lines when we do the final budget)**
- **IOF** (Brazil's tax on foreign-currency spending): the knowledge base indicates 3.5% for card and currency purchases (checked October 2, 2026). Rates change by decree, so **confirm the current rate with the Receita Federal or your bank**.
- Card spread (usually 4% to 6% on traditional credit cards; usually lower on global accounts).
- Tourist tax per night (roughly €1 to €10 per person, charged at the hotel), checked baggage and seat selection, airport transfer, eSIM.

**If it doesn't fit, concrete ways to make it work**
1. Low or shoulder season (lodging can drop 20% to 40%; shoulder season is April to May and September to October).
2. Fewer cities (for 12 days, Lisbon plus Porto, or Lisbon plus day trips like Sintra, is comfortable).
3. An apartment with a kitchen, in a neighborhood well served by the metro.
4. Use miles for the flights if you have them.

**How to pay (neutral recommendation):** a global account or prepaid card in foreign currency (buying currency gradually over the 3 to 6 months before), a backup credit card and a little cash.
