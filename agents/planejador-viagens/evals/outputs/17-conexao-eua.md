**Yes, you need something, and it is a big something: a US visa.** A connection at a US airport requires a **B1/B2 visa for Brazilians, even if you never leave the international area.** The ESTA doesn't work for Brazilians either, so there is no quick substitute.

**What that means for this ticket**
- If you don't have a valid US visa, the airline will probably refuse boarding in São Paulo, so this cheap ticket would be money lost.
- If you don't have a visa yet: the process involves the DS-160 form, the consular fee, biometrics at the CASV and an interview. Since September 2025 the interview waiver is much more restricted, and wait times vary from weeks to many months depending on the city. Check the wait time on **travel.state.gov**. **Only buy tickets after the visa is approved.** A 3-hour connection is not worth a visa process.
- If you already have a valid B1/B2 visa, you can transit, but note that it is a short connection for an international arrival with immigration and a security check: ask the airline about the minimum connection time and baggage handling.

**My recommendation:** look for a São Paulo to Lisbon flight **without a US connection**: a direct flight, or a connection in Europe, Brazil or another country. The price difference is usually smaller than the cost and risk of the visa.
