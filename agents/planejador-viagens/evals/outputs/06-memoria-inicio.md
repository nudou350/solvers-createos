**Good news on documents:** Chile is an associate of Mercosur, so Brazilians can enter as tourists with a passport, or with a **physical** RG or the new CIN. The RG must be in good condition and issued **at most 10 years ago**; the CNH (driver's license) and the digital RG/CIN in the gov.br app are not accepted. No visa is needed for tourism. I'll confirm this against the official sources (Itamaraty Consular Portal, gov.br/mre) when we get to the documents.

**Timing hints that depend on your answers**
- Patagonia (Torres del Paine) has its main season from November to March; outside it many services close.
- Atacama is pleasant almost all year, but the "altiplanic winter" can bring rain and cancel tours in January and February.
- Atacama is above 2,400 m, so I would plan 1 to 2 light days to acclimatize.
- Brazilian school vacations (January and July) raise prices for Santiago and Chile in general.
