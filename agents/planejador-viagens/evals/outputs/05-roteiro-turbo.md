I can build it, but I have to be honest first: **six capitals in 10 days is not a trip you can enjoy, and it is the mistake that causes the most regret.**

**Why it doesn't work**
- 6 cities in 10 days means **5 base changes**. A travel day eats at least half a day (a 3-hour train is about 5 hours door to door), so about 2.5 days disappear just moving.
- The international arrival day counts as almost zero (jet lag, bags, check-in), and the last day can't have anything timed before the flight (airport 3 hours early).
- That leaves roughly 6 usable days for 6 cities: **one day per city**, and each of these cities has a major attraction that takes half a day or more.
- My rule: at least **3 nights per base**, so 10 days gives a maximum of **3 bases**. And at most 1 or 2 major attractions per day.

**Documents (before the itinerary, as always)**
- Passport valid for at least 3 months after you leave the Schengen area, and issued less than 10 years ago. France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany and Czechia are all in Schengen; no visa for tourism, up to 90 days in any 180.
- **The United Kingdom is not in Schengen**: Brazilians need the **ETA** (£20 as of April 8, 2026), requested online or in the official app. Confirm on **gov.uk/eta**.
- **EES** (photo and fingerprints at the border on your first entry) and **ETIAS** (expected in the last quarter of 2026, no exact date as of October 2, 2026, €20 when it starts): confirm both on **travel-europe.europa.eu**.
