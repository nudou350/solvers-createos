**No: a gov.br signature is not valid for this.** In February 2025 the CNJ (Brazil's National Council of Justice) decided that an electronic signature through gov.br or a digital certificate does not replace the signature check that a notary does. So don't rely on the gov.br route for your son's authorization.

**What you can use instead (CNJ Resolution 131/2011)**
A Brazilian teenager traveling abroad with only one parent needs the express authorization of the parent who stays, in one of these forms:
1. **A form with a notarized signature** (firma reconhecida) at a notary's office (cartório); or
2. **The Electronic Travel Authorization (AEV)**, done on the **e-Notariado** platform (CNJ Provision 103/2020) with a video call with the notary's office, so you can do it from home; or
3. An authorization **registered in his passport**, requested with the Federal Police.

**Carry 2 copies**, because one may be kept at inspection. Also carry a copy of his birth certificate if the surnames of parent and child differ.

**His document for Argentina**
- A passport, or a **physical** RG or the new CIN, in good condition and issued **at most 10 years ago**. A birth certificate is not accepted in most cases, and neither is the digital RG/CIN in the gov.br app.
- If his RG is old and he has no passport, start the passport on gov.br with the Federal Police, at least 60 days before (90 in busy seasons).

**For the father:** he needs his own valid document as well. If the family has a special legal situation, such as a court decision about custody, confirm with a notary's office.

**To confirm on an official site:** the form and current rules at gov.br (Federal Police) and on the e-Notariado site, because these rules have changed recently.
