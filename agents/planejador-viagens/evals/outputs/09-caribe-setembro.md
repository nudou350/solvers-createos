Congratulations! Honest answer: **September is not the ideal month for a Caribbean honeymoon.**

**Why**
- The Atlantic hurricane season runs from **June to November 30**, and it **peaks between August and October**. September is right in the worst of it. That means a real chance of storms, rain, canceled excursions, or a changed flight.
- December to April is the dry, sunny season in the Caribbean, but it is also the more expensive one.

I am not saying you can't go. Many people do, and it can be cheaper. If September is a fixed date (the wedding date, vacation days), then these protections make sense:
1. **Insurance with trip cancellation and interruption cover**, bought right after the flight, and read the conditions about weather events.
2. **Refundable or free-cancellation lodging**, and a flight fare with clear rebooking rules.
3. A resort with a good reputation for handling storms, and flexibility on the dates (a few extra days of margin).
4. Be ready for a plan B: a few indoor activities, spa, and an emergency reserve of at least 10% of the budget.

**Alternatives for a September honeymoon that I would consider**
- **Europe in shoulder season** (April to May and September to October are the best value for money), for example Portugal, Italy or Greece. Late September is milder than summer.
- **Northeast Brazil**: September to March is usually drier and sunnier (the rainy season for Salvador, Recife, Maceió and João Pessoa is April to July).
- **Buenos Aires** (spring starts in September) if you want something romantic with city life.
- If your heart is set on the Caribbean: move to **December to April** (book months ahead, it is expensive).
- Avoid Japan in this period if you were considering it: typhoon season runs from August to October.

**Documents:** my knowledge base does not cover the entry rules of the Dominican Republic in detail. I can't state them as certain: please check the **Itamaraty Consular Portal (gov.br/mre)** and the Dominican consulate for visa, tourist card, health requirements and passport validity (as a rule of thumb, at least 6 months of validity). Each traveler also needs a valid passport, and for a honeymoon, make sure the **name on the airline ticket matches the passport exactly**, which matters if you plan to change your name after the wedding.
