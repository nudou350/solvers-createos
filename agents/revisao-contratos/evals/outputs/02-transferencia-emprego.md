**Good news: a job transfer can mean zero penalty, if you follow two conditions.**

Under art. 4, sole paragraph, of Law 8.245/1991, the tenant does not pay the early termination penalty when:

1. the exit results from a **transfer by the employer** (private or public) to work in **another location**, different from where the contract began (Campinas to Recife fits); **and**
2. you **notify the landlord in writing at least 30 days in advance**.

Because you have two months, you have time, but the 30 days count backward from the date you return the property, so do not wait.

**What to do, in order**

1. **Get proof of the transfer** from your company: a letter, an e-mail from HR, an official order or the new employment contract.
2. **Send the written notice now** (or as soon as you have the proof), with a move-out date at least 30 days after the notice. Use e-mail with a reply, registered letter with return receipt or a notice through a notary's office, and keep the proof. Suggested text:

   > "[City], [date]. Dear [name of landlord or agency]. I am writing to inform you that my employer has transferred me to work in Recife, as shown in the attached document. For that reason I will return the property at [address] on [date, at least 30 days ahead], and I am exempt from the penalty under the sole paragraph of art. 4 of Law 8.245/1991. Please schedule the move-out inspection. Kind regards, [name]."

3. **Ask for the move-out inspection** with both parties present, with photos and a signed key handover form (the guarantee usually lasts until the keys are actually returned, art. 39).
4. **Ask for the return of the security deposit**, if you gave one, with the savings account earnings (art. 38, par. 2).

**If the waiver is refused or does not apply**

Without the 30-day notice, the waiver can be challenged. The rule speaks of an employer transfer: a move you choose yourself, or work as a self-employed person, is not covered. In that case you would pay the proportional penalty (art. 4), estimated here:

- Full penalty: 3 x rent (tell me the rent for the exact figure).
- Months remaining if you leave at month 10: 30 - 10 = 20 of 30.
- Proportional penalty = 3 x rent x 20 / 30, which is two thirds of three months' rent, or **2 months' rent**. (At month 8, it would be 3 x rent x 22 / 30.)

For example, with a R$ 2,000 rent, leaving at month 10 would be R$ 4,000. These are estimates (and a judge can reduce an excessive penalty, Civil Code, art. 413).

**Questions to confirm**

- Is the transfer an order from your employer (not a request from you)?
- What is the current rent, and is there a deposit or other guarantee?
- Does the contract say anything about transfers, notice or the penalty base?
