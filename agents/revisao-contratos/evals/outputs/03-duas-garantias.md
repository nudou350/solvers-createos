**Short answer: something is wrong. You should not accept both.**

The Tenancy Law allows **only one type of guarantee per contract** (Law 8.245/1991, art. 37, sole paragraph). A guarantor **and** a cash security deposit are two types, so the combination is prohibited, on pain of nullity.

| Item | Classification | Legal basis | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guarantor + 3-month cash deposit together | **ILLEGAL** | art. 37, sole paragraph; art. 45 | Ask for a single guarantee |
| Cash deposit of 3 months' rent (on its own) | OK (it is exactly the limit) | art. 38, par. 2 | Check it goes into a savings account |

**Why it matters**

- Requiring more than one guarantee is a **criminal misdemeanor** (art. 43, II): simple imprisonment of 5 days to 6 months or a fine of 3 to 12 months' rent, paid to the tenant. The extra guarantee is void, and you can ask for what was given in excess back.
- You choose among the guarantees the law lists (art. 37): a **guarantor**, a **cash deposit** (up to 3 months' rent), **rental guarantee insurance** (art. 41, premium paid by you) or fiduciary assignment of investment fund shares. Having two guarantors is allowed (it is still one type).

**If you choose the deposit (art. 38, par. 2):**

- at most 3 months' rent;
- held in a **savings account**, with the **earnings belonging to you** when it is returned;
- ask the contract to state the deadline for returning it after the move-out inspection.

**If you choose the guarantor:** they must know that their family home can be seized for rent debt (Law 8.009/1990, art. 3, VII; STJ Precedent 549), and they are not liable for amendments they did not sign (STJ Precedent 214).

**Message you can send to the agency**

> "Hello, [name]. I am interested in the property at [address]. The draft asks for a guarantor and also a 3-month security deposit. Under art. 37, sole paragraph, of Law 8.245/1991, only one type of guarantee is allowed per contract. Can we keep only [guarantor / deposit]? Suggested wording: 'The lease is guaranteed exclusively by [type], under art. 37 of Law 8.245/1991.' Thank you."
