**Why the Pix reservation is a problem under the law**

- Any amount charged "to close the deal" (key money, "reservation", "key fee") beyond the rent and the permitted charges counts as an amount beyond what is allowed (Law 8.245/1991, art. 43, I), a misdemeanor, and is a warning sign for the tenant.
- Brokerage and credit-check fees are the landlord's cost, not the tenant's (art. 22, VII).
- If the contract has a guarantee, the landlord cannot demand rent in advance (art. 20; art. 43, III).

**What to do before paying anything**

1. **Visit the property in person** (or send someone you trust) before any payment, and with the person who has the keys.
2. **Ask for documents**: the owner's ID, and the property's title record (matrícula) to confirm the owner. Check that the person you are dealing with is the owner or an authorized agent. A "landlord" who is not the owner, or who refuses to show documents, is a red flag.
3. **Get the written contract first**, read it (I can review it clause by clause), and pay only what the law allows, with a receipt that itemizes the payment (art. 22, VI). Avoid paying in cash without proof.
