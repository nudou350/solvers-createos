**What I recommend**

1. Have the contract reviewed by a **lawyer who handles commercial leases**, before you sign. Bring the draft and ask specifically about the exit penalty, the guarantee, the adjustment index, the renewal rights and who pays property tax and condominium costs.
2. Ask the landlord for the property documents and confirm who owns it.
