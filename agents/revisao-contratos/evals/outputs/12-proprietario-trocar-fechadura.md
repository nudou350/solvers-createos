**What you can do**

Non-payment of rent and charges is grounds for ending the lease (art. 9, III). The lawful route is:

1. **Collect the debt in writing.** Send an itemized statement (rent, charges, fine and interest from the contract) with proof of delivery. Offer a written payment plan if you prefer a settlement.
2. **File an eviction action (ação de despejo).** A lawyer will handle it.
   - In some cases the judge grants an injunction to vacate in 15 days without hearing the tenant, with a 3-month rent deposit posted by you, including **non-payment in a contract with no guarantee at all** (art. 59, par. 1, IX). With a guarantee, the normal procedure applies.
   - The tenant can **avoid termination** by depositing the updated debt in court within 15 days from the summons: rent and charges, contractual fines, interest, court costs and 10% fees (art. 62, II). They cannot do this if they already did so in the previous 24 months (art. 62, sole paragraph).
   - After the ruling, the voluntary move-out period is generally 15 days when the eviction is for non-payment (art. 63, par. 1), then eviction is carried out (art. 65).
3. **Activate the guarantee** according to its type (guarantor, insurance or deposit), with a lawyer's help.
4. **Keep the evidence:** the contract, the move-in inspection report, the payment history and all messages.

**Next steps**
