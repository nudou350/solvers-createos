**Short answer: no. Both parts are a problem.**

Under Law 8.245/1991, art. 38, par. 2, a cash security deposit:

- **cannot exceed 3 months' rent**; and
- must be **held in a savings account**, with the **earnings belonging to the tenant** when it is returned.

| Item | Classification | Legal basis | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deposit of 5 months' rent | **ILLEGAL** | art. 38, par. 2; art. 45 | Reduce to at most 3 months |
| Held by the agency, earning nothing | **ILLEGAL** | art. 38, par. 2 | Require a savings account, earnings to you |

**The numbers (rent R$ 2,200)**

- Maximum deposit: 3 x R$ 2,200 = **R$ 6,600.00**
- What they ask: 5 x R$ 2,200 = **R$ 11,000.00**
- Excess above the cap: 11,000 - 6,600 = **R$ 4,400.00**

Calculated on the rent alone. Charges are not part of the cap base.

**Also check**

- If you are also asked for a guarantor or rental guarantee insurance, that is a second guarantee and prohibited (art. 37, sole paragraph; art. 43, II).
- Ask for the **deadline for returning the deposit** after the move-out inspection, and that only proven debts are deducted.

**Message you can send**

> "Hello, [name]. The draft asks for a cash deposit of 5 months' rent (R$ 11,000.00) with no earnings. Under art. 38, par. 2 of Law 8.245/1991, the deposit is limited to 3 months' rent (R$ 6,600.00) and must be placed in a savings account, with the earnings going to the tenant. Can we adjust the clause? Suggested wording: 'The TENANT will give a cash security deposit of 3 months' rent, held in a savings account in [name or joint account], with the earnings to the TENANT, and returned within [30] days after the move-out inspection.' Thank you."

**Next steps**

1. Send the request in writing and keep the answer.
2. If you already paid, ask in writing for the excess (R$ 4,400.00) to be returned. For a refusal, seek a lawyer, the Public Defender's Office (Defensoria Pública) or the Small Claims Court (Juizado Especial Cível).
3. If the agency insists, consider a different guarantee or another property. A review of the rest of the contract is also available.
