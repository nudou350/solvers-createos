**Short answer: about R$ 3,600, not the full R$ 5,400.**

Under art. 4 of Law 8.245/1991, a tenant may leave early by paying the agreed penalty, but the penalty must be **proportional to the part of the contract already served**. A clause demanding the full penalty with no proportionality contradicts art. 4 and is void in that part (art. 45).

**The calculation (using your numbers)**

- Full penalty: 3 months x R$ 1,800 = **R$ 5,400**
- Months remaining: 30 - 10 = **20**
- Proportional penalty: 5,400 x 20 / 30 = **R$ 3,600.00**

For comparison, if you left at a different point of the same contract:

| Month you leave | Months remaining | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| 10 (now) | 20 | R$ 3,600.00 |
| 12 | 18 | R$ 3,240.00 |
| 15 | 15 | R$ 2,700.00 |
| 20 | 10 | R$ 1,800.00 |

(Staying longer lowers the penalty, but each extra month costs you R$ 1,800 in rent, so waiting rarely pays off.)

These are estimates based on the law, not an exact bill. The assumptions are: the penalty is on the **current rent** (R$ 1,800 is the current amount, with no adjustment pending) and **charges such as condominium and IPTU are not in the base**, unless the clause says "rent and charges". A judge can also reduce a manifestly excessive penalty (Civil Code, art. 413).

**Two ways you might pay nothing**

- If you are leaving because **your employer transferred you to another location**, and you give **written notice at least 30 days in advance**, there is no penalty (art. 4, sole paragraph). Keep the employer's letter.
- That does not apply if the contract term had already ended (then art. 6 applies instead), but at month 10 of 30 you are still inside the term.

**What to do**

1. Check the exact wording of the penalty clause: does it say "current rent"? "proportional"? "rent and charges"?
2. Give notice **in writing**, with proof of delivery (e-mail with reply, registered letter or notary notice), and ask for the move-out inspection.
3. If the landlord or agency demands the full R$ 5,400, reply in writing citing art. 4 and offer the proportional amount: "Under art. 4 of Law 8.245/1991, the penalty is proportional to the time remaining; I calculate R$ 3,600.00 (3 x R$ 1,800 x 20/30)."
4. Ask for the security deposit back, if any, within the deadline set in the contract (art. 38, par. 2).
