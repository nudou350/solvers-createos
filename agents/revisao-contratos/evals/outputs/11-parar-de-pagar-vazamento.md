**Short answer: I advise against stopping payment.** Non-payment of rent is grounds for ending the lease (Law 8.245/1991, art. 9, III), and it opens the way to an eviction action, even if the landlord is the one who failed. There are better tools.

**What the law provides about the defect**

- The landlord must deliver the property in usable condition (art. 22, I) and answers for defects that **predate** the lease (art. 22, IV). Damage caused by you, your family or visitors is the tenant's (art. 23, V), so first check whether the leak was caused by use or comes from the building (pipes, structure).
- **Urgent repairs** the landlord is responsible for must be allowed by the tenant (art. 26). If they last **more than 10 days**, the tenant is entitled to a **proportional rent reduction** for the excess period, and if they last **more than 30 days**, the tenant can end the contract (art. 26, sole paragraph). Your leak has lasted two months. I did not find a specific rule in my knowledge base about a landlord who simply does not start the repair, so whether art. 26 applies in that exact situation is something to **confirm with a lawyer**.
- **Necessary improvements**, which prevent the property from deteriorating (for example, fixing a leak that threatens the structure), are reimbursable even without the landlord's authorization, with a right of retention (art. 35), unless the contract says otherwise. Keep invoices and photos, and notify the landlord before acting.

**What to do, in order**

1. **Notify the landlord or agency in writing**, with photos, and with proof of delivery. Template:
   > "Dear [name]. On [date] I identified [description of the leak, with photos attached] at the property at [address]. The problem [already existed at move-in, as shown in the move-in inspection | appeared on date]. I request an inspection and repair within [reasonable period] and I will allow access for the repair. Kind regards, [name]."
2. **Keep paying the rent**, on time, with proof of payment.
3. **Document everything**: dates, photos, messages, the move-in inspection report (art. 22, V).
4. If there is no answer, seek the **Defensoria Pública, a lawyer or the Small Claims Court (Juizado Especial Cível)** to ask for the repair and a rent reduction, and ask whether you can do the work yourself and be reimbursed.
