I cannot draft a complete lease, and I will not write those two clauses as requested, because they would be void and could expose **you** as the landlord. What I can do is show what is legal and still protects you, and give you wording for the key clauses.

**The two requests, classified**

| Request | Classification | Legal basis | Consequence for you |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 full months' rent penalty for early exit | **ILLEGAL in the "full" part** | Law 8.245/1991, art. 4; art. 45; Civil Code, art. 413 | The penalty must be proportional to the time served; a penalty above 3 months is attention and can be reduced as manifestly excessive |
| Guarantor + 3-month cash deposit | **ILLEGAL** | art. 37, sole paragraph; art. 43, II | The extra guarantee is void and demanding it is a misdemeanor: simple imprisonment of 5 days to 6 months or a fine of 3 to 12 months' rent, paid to the tenant |

**What does protect you (and is legal)**

1. **One guarantee, chosen well** (art. 37): a guarantor, rental guarantee insurance (premium paid by the tenant, art. 23, XI) or a cash deposit of up to 3 months' rent in a savings account with earnings to the tenant (art. 38, par. 2). Two guarantors count as one type. Ask whether the guarantor owns property.
2. **A proportional early termination penalty.** The law does not fix the full amount; 3 months' rent is the market custom. Wording:
   > "In case of early return of the property, the TENANT will pay a penalty of [3] months of current rent, reduced proportionally to the period of the contract already served (art. 4 of Law 8.245/1991), waived in the case of the sole paragraph of the same article."
3. **Late payment:** a 10% fine and 1% per month interest are usually accepted. Higher percentages are attention (Civil Code, arts. 406, 412 and 413). The CDC 2% cap does not apply between private individuals (STJ case law).
4. **IPTU and fire insurance to the tenant** by an express clause (art. 22, VIII). Extraordinary condominium expenses stay with you (art. 22, X).
5. **Annual adjustment** by a price index (Law 10.192/2001, art. 2), with a replacement index and a rule for negative changes.
6. **Move-in inspection report** attached and signed (art. 22, V; art. 23, III): the best protection against damage disputes.
7. **Term:** a written 30-month contract allows termination without cause after expiry, with 30 days' notice (art. 46, par. 2).
8. **A validity clause** in case of sale, registered in the title record (matrícula) (art. 8), if relevant.
