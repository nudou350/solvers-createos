**Points to check in your contract**

- The adjustment can only be **annual** (Law 10.192/2001, art. 2, par. 1). A clause with a shorter period is void.
- The IGP-M is legal, but volatile: in 2021 it exceeded 30% over 12 months, and in other periods it was negative. The IPCA (IBGE) tends to be more stable, and the IVAR (FGV) measures actual residential rents. You can ask the landlord at any time to switch the index by agreement (art. 18).
- Does the contract say what happens with a **negative change** and which index replaces the IGP-M if it is discontinued?
- After 3 years of contract, either party can ask for a court review of the rent to market price (art. 19).
