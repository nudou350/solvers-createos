# Notice and request templates for residential leases (Brazil)

These templates follow the Brazilian Tenancy Law (Law 8.245/1991). Fill in the fields in square brackets. Send everything **in writing** and with proof of delivery (e-mail with a reply, registered letter with return receipt or notice through a notary's office) and keep a copy. Do not put ID numbers (CPF) or bank details in the messages: if the recipient asks, agree on another channel.

> These templates are a starting point and do not replace the advice of a lawyer. In high-risk cases (eviction, lawsuit, high amounts), seek a lawyer or the Public Defender's Office (Defensoria Pública) before sending.

## 1. Notice of return of the property in an indefinite-term contract (art. 6)

Use it when the contract term has already ended and you are still in the property.

"[City], [date]. Dear [name of landlord or agency]. I am writing to inform you that I will vacate the property at [address] on [date, at least 30 days ahead], under art. 6 of Law 8.245/1991. Please schedule the move-out inspection for [suggested date] and let me know the deadline for returning the security deposit, if any. Kind regards, [name]."

Without this notice, the landlord can charge one month of rent and charges (art. 6, sole paragraph).

## 2. Notice of leaving because of a job transfer (art. 4, sole paragraph)

It only applies if your employer (private or public) transferred you to work in another location. Attach the employer's letter or announcement.

"[City], [date]. Dear [name]. I am writing to inform you that my employer has transferred me to work in [destination city], as shown in the attached document. For that reason I will return the property at [address] on [date, at least 30 days ahead], and I am exempt from the penalty under the sole paragraph of art. 4 of Law 8.245/1991. Please schedule the move-out inspection. Kind regards, [name]."

## 3. Request to fix a clause before signing

"Dear [name]. I have read the draft contract for the property at [address] and I am interested in going ahead. To bring it in line with Law 8.245/1991, I ask for the following points to be adjusted: [clause and article, for example: single guarantee, art. 37, sole paragraph; proportional penalty, art. 4]. Suggested wording: [proposed text]. I am available to talk. Kind regards, [name]."

## 4. Request for return of the security deposit after the keys are handed over

"Dear [name]. I handed over the keys of the property at [address] on [date], as shown in the signed handover form and the move-out inspection. I request the return of the security deposit of R$ [amount], with the savings account earnings (art. 38, par. 2 of Law 8.245/1991), within [period set in the contract] days, deducting only proven debts. I also ask for the deposit statement. Kind regards, [name]."

## 5. Request for a validity clause and registration (protection if the property is sold, art. 8)

"Dear [name]. I ask that the contract include the validity clause in case the property is sold and that the contract be registered in the property's title record (matrícula), so that the buyer respects the agreed term (art. 8 of Law 8.245/1991). To register it, the contract must have two witnesses. Can we agree on who pays the notary's office? Kind regards, [name]."

## 6. Notice of a defect or repair to the landlord

"Dear [name]. On [date] I identified [description of the problem, with photos attached] at the property at [address]. The problem [already existed at move-in, as shown in the move-in inspection | appeared on date]. I request an inspection and repair within [reasonable period] and I will allow access for the repair. Kind regards, [name]."

Do not stop paying rent because of the defect: non-payment is grounds for eviction (art. 9, III).
