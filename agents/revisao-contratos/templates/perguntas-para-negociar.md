# Template: questions to negotiate the rental contract

Use only the questions that apply to your contract. Send them in writing (e-mail or message) and keep the answers. Suggested tone: polite and objective. These questions assume a residential lease in Brazil, under the Brazilian Tenancy Law (Law 8.245/1991).

> This review does not replace the advice of a lawyer.

## Opening message

"Hello, [name]. I have read the draft contract for the property at [address] and I am interested in going ahead. Before signing, I would like to adjust a few points so the contract complies with the Tenancy Law (Law 8.245/1991):"

## Tenant questions

1. **Guarantee (art. 37, sole paragraph):** "The contract asks for [guarantor and security deposit]. The law allows only one type of guarantee. Can we keep only [preferred option]?"
2. **Security deposit (art. 38, par. 2):** "Can the deposit be placed in a savings account in my name or a joint account, with the earnings going to me, and be returned within [30] days after the move-out inspection?"
3. **Early termination penalty (art. 4):** "Can we state expressly that the penalty of [3] months' rent will be proportional to the months remaining, and that there is no penalty if my employer transfers me (art. 4, sole paragraph)?"
4. **Rent adjustment (Law 10.192/2001, art. 2):** "Can we use the IPCA instead of the IGP-M, with an annual adjustment and without applying a negative change as an increase?"
5. **Advance rent (art. 20):** "Since the contract already has a guarantee, can the first rent fall due in the month after use, as the law provides?"
6. **Fees (art. 22, VII):** "The contract/registration/brokerage fee is the landlord's charge. Can we remove it?"
7. **Condominium (art. 22, X):** "Can we make clear that extraordinary expenses (works, facade, reserve fund) stay with the landlord?"
8. **Inspection (art. 22, V):** "Can we do the move-in inspection together, with photos, and attach it to the contract?"
9. **Sale of the property (art. 8):** "Can we include a validity clause in case of sale and register the contract in the property's title record?"
10. **Improvements (art. 35):** "If I need to make a necessary repair, how will reimbursement work? Can we provide for written authorization for improvements?"
11. **Visits (art. 23, IX):** "Can visits to the property be arranged for a set day and time, with [24/48] hours' notice?"

## Questions for the landlord (individual)

1. **Single guarantee (art. 37, sole paragraph):** "Which guarantee do you prefer: guarantor, rental guarantee insurance or a deposit of up to 3 months' rent?"
2. **Guarantor (art. 40, X; STJ Precedent 214):** "Can the guarantor also sign any amendments? Do they own property in their name?"
3. **Inspection (arts. 22, V and 23, III):** "Shall we prepare the move-in report together, with photos, to avoid arguments at move-out?"
4. **Term (arts. 46 and 47):** "Would you prefer a 30-month contract? For shorter contracts, after expiry the property can only be taken back in the cases the law provides."
5. **IPTU and insurance (art. 22, VIII):** "Will the IPTU and fire insurance be paid by the tenant? We need to state this expressly."

## Proposed alternative wording

- **Proportional penalty:** "In case of early return of the property, the TENANT will pay a penalty of [3] months of current rent, reduced proportionally to the period of the contract already served (art. 4 of Law 8.245/1991), waived in the case of the sole paragraph of the same article."
- **Rent adjustment:** "The rent will be adjusted every 12 months by the cumulative change in the IPCA/IBGE; in case of a negative change, the rent will remain unchanged."
- **Single guarantee:** "The lease is guaranteed exclusively by [type], under art. 37 of Law 8.245/1991."
