# Rental contract clause review table

Key: **OK** = complies with the law · **ATTENTION** = legal, but negotiate or understand the risk · **ILLEGAL/ABUSIVE** = contradicts the law (void) or is excessive.

> [If high risk: put the notice to seek a lawyer or the Public Defender's Office (Defensoria Pública) here, BEFORE the table.]

## What is missing from the contract

- [ ] Move-in inspection report attached (art. 22, V)
- [ ] Validity clause in case of sale (art. 8)
- [ ] Replacement index if the index is discontinued
- [ ] Deadline for returning the deposit
