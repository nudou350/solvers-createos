# Other laws cited in the lease review: Civil Code, Law 10.192/2001, Law 14.905/2024 and the Usury Law

> Updated October 2026. General guidance on urban residential leases in Brazil (Law 8.245/1991), not legal advice: it does not replace the advice of a lawyer or the Public Defender's Office (Defensoria Pública).

Text of the provisions as published in the compilations of the Presidency of the Republic (planalto.gov.br), consulted on 2026-09-30: Civil Code (Law 10.406/2002, compiled text, as worded by Law 14.905/2024), Law 10.192/2001, Law 14.905/2024 and Decree 22.626/1933. The wording below is an unofficial English translation of the official Portuguese text; the Portuguese original is the only authoritative version. The reference to "art. n. 1.062" in Decree 22.626 points to the 1916 Civil Code; today the legal rate is in art. 406 of the 2002 Civil Code.

## Civil Code, art. 389: damages, interest and IPCA adjustment

Art. 389. If the obligation is not performed, the debtor is liable for losses and damages, plus interest, inflation adjustment and lawyer's fees. (Wording given by Law No. 14.905 of 2024)

Sole paragraph. If the inflation adjustment index was not agreed or is not provided for in specific law, the change in the Broad National Consumer Price Index (IPCA), calculated and published by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE), or in the index that replaces it, will apply. (Added by Law No. 14.905 of 2024)

## Civil Code, art. 406: legal interest rate (Selic minus IPCA)

Art. 406. When interest was not agreed, or was agreed without a stipulated rate, or arises from a determination of law, interest will be set according to the legal rate. (Wording given by Law No. 14.905 of 2024)

Par. 1. The legal rate will correspond to the reference rate of the Special Settlement and Custody System (Selic), less the inflation adjustment index referred to in the sole paragraph of art. 389 of this Code. (Added by Law No. 14.905 of 2024)

Par. 2. The methodology for calculating the legal rate and the way it is applied will be defined by the National Monetary Council and published by the Central Bank of Brazil. (Added by Law No. 14.905 of 2024)

Par. 3. If the legal rate results in a negative figure, it will be deemed equal to 0 (zero) for the purpose of calculating interest in the reference period. (Added by Law No. 14.905 of 2024)

## Civil Code, arts. 408, 411, 412 and 413: penalty clause and reduction of an excessive fine

Art. 408. The debtor incurs the penalty clause by operation of law as soon as, through fault, they fail to perform the obligation or fall into default.

Art. 411. When the penalty clause is stipulated for the case of default, or as special security for another specific clause, the creditor may demand payment of the stipulated penalty together with performance of the main obligation.

Art. 412. The amount of the penalty imposed in the penalty clause cannot exceed that of the main obligation.

Art. 413. The penalty must be reduced equitably by the judge if the main obligation has been performed in part, or if the amount of the penalty is manifestly excessive, taking into account the nature and purpose of the transaction.

## Civil Code, arts. 423 and 424: standard-form contracts

Art. 423. When a standard-form contract (contrato de adesão) contains ambiguous or contradictory clauses, the interpretation most favorable to the adhering party must be adopted.

Art. 424. In standard-form contracts, clauses that stipulate an advance waiver by the adhering party of a right resulting from the nature of the transaction are void.

## Law 10.192/2001, art. 2: annual adjustment and nullity of more frequent adjustment

Art. 2. It is permitted to stipulate inflation adjustment or adjustment by general or sectoral price indexes, or indexes that reflect the change in production costs or in the inputs used, in contracts with a duration of one year or more.

Par. 1. Any stipulation of adjustment or inflation adjustment with a period shorter than one year is void by operation of law.

Par. 2. In case of contract revision, the starting point of the inflation adjustment or adjustment period, or of a new revision, will be the date on which the previous revision took place.

Par. 3. Subject to the provisions of par. 7 of art. 28 of Law No. 9.069 of June 29, 1995, and of the following paragraph, any devices that, in calculating the adjustment index, produce financial effects equivalent to an adjustment with a period shorter than annual are void by operation of law.

## Law 14.905/2024, art. 3: where the Usury Law stopped applying

Art. 3. Decree No. 22.626 of April 7, 1933 does not apply to obligations:

I - contracted between legal entities;

II - represented by credit instruments or securities;

III - incurred with:

a) financial institutions and other institutions authorized to operate by the Central Bank of Brazil;

b) investment funds or clubs;

c) leasing companies and simple credit companies;

d) civil society organizations of public interest governed by Law No. 9.790 of March 23, 1999, that are dedicated to granting credit; or

IV - carried out in the financial, capital or securities markets.

## Decree 22.626/1933 (Usury Law), art. 1: interest up to double the legal rate

Art. 1. It is prohibited, and will be punished under this law, to stipulate in any contract interest rates higher than double the legal rate (Civil Code, art. n. 1.062).
