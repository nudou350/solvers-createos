# Law 8.245/1991: penalties and nullities (arts. 43 to 45)

> Updated September 2026. This does not replace the advice of a lawyer.

Official text of Law No. 8.245 of October 18, 1991 (Brazilian Tenancy Law, "Lei do Inquilinato"), according to the compilation of the Presidency of the Republic (planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8245.htm), consulted on 2026-09-30. Repealed passages were omitted; the notes "(Wording given by...)" and "(Added by...)" come from the compilation itself. Latest amendments to this law up to the date consulted: Law 12.112/2009 and Law 12.744/2012. The wording below is an unofficial English translation of the official Portuguese text; the Portuguese original is the only authoritative version. Legal texts are not protected by copyright (Law 9.610/1998, art. 8, IV).

## Art. 43 of the Tenancy Law: criminal misdemeanor: undue charges, two guarantees and advance rent

Art. 43. It constitutes a criminal misdemeanor, punishable by simple imprisonment of five days to six months or a fine of three to twelve times the amount of the last updated rent, paid to the tenant:

I - to demand, by reason of a lease or sublease, an amount or value beyond the rent and the permitted charges;

II - to demand, by reason of a lease or sublease, more than one type of guarantee in the same lease contract;

III - to charge rent in advance, except in the case of art. 42 and short-stay rentals.

## Art. 44 of the Tenancy Law: crimes by the landlord

Art. 44. It constitutes a crime subject to public prosecution, punishable by detention of three months to one year, which may be replaced by community service:

I - for the landlord or sublessor, in multi-family collective housing, to refuse to provide an itemized receipt for rent and charges;

II - for the party taking the property back, within one hundred and eighty days after the property is handed over, in the case of item III of art. 47, to fail to use it for the declared purpose or, if using it, not to do so for a minimum of one year;

III - for the owner, promissory buyer or promissory assignee, in the cases of item IV of art. 9, item IV of art. 47, item I of art. 52 and item II of art. 53, not to begin the demolition or repair of the property within sixty days counted from its handover;

IV - to carry out the eviction without observing the provisions of par. 2 of art. 65.

Sole paragraph. If any of the cases provided for in this article occurs, the injured party can claim, in separate proceedings, a fine equivalent to a minimum of twelve and a maximum of twenty-four times the amount of the last updated rent or of what is being charged to the new tenant, if the property is re-let.

## Art. 45 of the Tenancy Law: void clauses

Art. 45. Clauses of the lease contract that aim to defeat the objectives of this law are void by operation of law, notably those that prohibit the extension provided for in art. 47, or that remove the right to renewal, in the case of art. 51, or that impose pecuniary obligations for that purpose.
