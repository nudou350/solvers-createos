# Law 8.245/1991: lease actions and eviction (arts. 58 to 66)

> Updated September 2026. This does not replace the advice of a lawyer.

Official text of Law No. 8.245 of October 18, 1991 (Brazilian Tenancy Law, "Lei do Inquilinato"), according to the compilation of the Presidency of the Republic (planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8245.htm), consulted on 2026-09-30. Repealed passages were omitted; the notes "(Wording given by...)" and "(Added by...)" come from the compilation itself. Latest amendments to this law up to the date consulted: Law 12.112/2009 and Law 12.744/2012. The wording below is an unofficial English translation of the official Portuguese text; the Portuguese original is the only authoritative version. Legal texts are not protected by copyright (Law 9.610/1998, art. 8, IV).

## Art. 58 of the Tenancy Law: general rules for lease actions (forum, value of the claim, service)

Art. 58. Except for the cases provided for in the sole paragraph of art. 1, in eviction actions, consignment actions for payment of rent and accessories of the lease, rent review actions and lease renewal actions, the following will be observed:

I - the proceedings continue during court holidays and are not suspended by them;

II - the court of the place where the property is located has jurisdiction to hear and judge such actions, unless another has been chosen in the contract;

III - the value of the claim will correspond to twelve months of rent, or, in the case of item II of art. 47, to three salaries in force at the time of filing;

IV - provided it is authorized in the contract, service of process, notice or notification will be made by mail with return receipt or, in the case of a legal entity or sole proprietorship, also by telex or fax, or, if necessary, by the other means provided for in the Code of Civil Procedure;

V - appeals filed against judgments will have devolutive effect only.

## Art. 59 of the Tenancy Law: eviction action and 15-day injunction

Art. 59. With the modifications contained in this chapter, eviction actions will follow the ordinary procedure.

Par. 1. An injunction to vacate in fifteen days will be granted, regardless of a hearing of the opposing party and provided a deposit equivalent to three months' rent is posted, in actions founded exclusively on:

I - breach of a mutual agreement (art. 9, item I), made in writing and signed by the parties and two witnesses, in which a minimum term of six months to vacate was agreed, counted from the signing of the instrument;

II - the provisions of item II of art. 47, with written proof of the termination of the employment contract or with it being shown at a preliminary hearing;

III - the end of the term of a short-stay rental, with the eviction action filed within thirty days after the contract expires;

IV - the death of the tenant leaving no legitimate successor to the lease, according to item I of art. 11, with persons not authorized by law remaining in the property;

V - the subtenant's continued stay in the property, once the lease entered into with the tenant has ended.

### Art. 59, par. 1, items VI to IX of the Tenancy Law: 15-day injunction: urgent repairs, new guarantee and non-payment without a guarantee

Art. 59, par. 1 (continued). VI – the provisions of item IV of art. 9, where there is a need to carry out urgent repairs on the property, ordered by the public authority, that cannot normally be carried out with the tenant remaining, or which, if they can, the tenant refuses to allow; (Added by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

VII – the end of the notice period provided for in the sole paragraph of art. 40, without presentation of a new guarantee capable of maintaining the initial security of the contract; (Added by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

VIII – the end of the term of a non-residential lease, with the action filed within 30 (thirty) days of the term or of compliance with a notification communicating the intent to take the property back; (Added by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

IX – non-payment of rent and accessories of the lease on the due date, when the contract lacks any of the guarantees provided for in art. 37, because it was not contracted or because it was extinguished or release from it was requested, regardless of the reason. (Added by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

### Art. 59, pars. 2 and 3 of the Tenancy Law: subtenants and court deposit to avoid the injunction

Art. 59, par. 2. Whatever the grounds of the action, subtenants will be notified of the request, and they can intervene in the proceedings as assistants.

Par. 3. In the case of item IX of par. 1 of this article, the tenant can avoid termination of the lease and defeat the injunction to vacate if, within the 15 (fifteen) days granted to vacate the property and regardless of calculation, they make a court deposit covering the totality of the amounts owed, in the manner provided for in item II of art. 62. (Added by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

## Art. 60 of the Tenancy Law: proof of ownership in the eviction action

Art. 60. In eviction actions founded on item IV of art. 9, item IV of art. 47 and item II of art. 53, the initial petition must be accompanied by proof of ownership of the property or of the registered promise.

## Art. 61 of the Tenancy Law: agreement to vacate: six-month period

Art. 61. In actions founded on par. 2 of art. 46 and on items III and IV of art. 47, if the tenant, within the period for the defense, expresses their agreement to vacate the property, the judge will grant the request, setting a period of six months to vacate, counted from service of process, imposing on the losing party responsibility for court costs and lawyer's fees of twenty percent of the value of the claim. If the property is vacated within the set period, the defendant will be exempt from this responsibility; otherwise, an eviction order will be issued.

## Art. 62 of the Tenancy Law: eviction for non-payment and curing the default

Art. 62. In eviction actions founded on non-payment of rent and accessories of the lease, of provisional rent, of differences in rent, or only of any of the accessories of the lease, the following will be observed: (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

I – the request for termination of the lease can be combined with the request for collection of the rent and accessories of the lease; in this case, the tenant will be summoned to respond to the request for termination and the tenant and the guarantors to respond to the request for collection, and an itemized calculation of the amount of the debt must be presented with the initial petition; (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

II – the tenant and the guarantor can avoid termination of the lease by paying, within 15 (fifteen) days, counted from service of process, the updated debt, regardless of calculation and by court deposit, including: (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

a) the rent and accessories of the lease that fall due until it is made;

b) the contractual fines or penalties, when enforceable;

c) default interest;

d) court costs and the landlord's lawyer's fees, set at ten percent of the amount owed, if the contract does not provide otherwise;

### Art. 62, items III to VI and sole paragraph of the Tenancy Law: topping up the deposit, rent during the proceedings and the 24-month limit

Art. 62 (continued). III – once the default has been cured, if the landlord claims that the offer is not complete, justifying the difference, the tenant can top up the deposit within 10 (ten) days, counted from the notice, which can be addressed to the tenant or directly to their lawyer, by letter or publication in the official gazette, at the landlord's request; (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

IV – if the deposit is not fully topped up, the request for termination will proceed for the difference, and the landlord can withdraw the amount deposited; (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

V - the rent that falls due until the judgment must be deposited at the court's disposal, on the respective due dates, and the landlord can withdraw it as long as it is uncontested;

VI - when the requests for termination of the lease and collection of rent are combined, enforcement of the latter can begin before the property is vacated, if both have been upheld.

Sole paragraph. Curing the default will not be admitted if the tenant has already used this option in the 24 (twenty-four) months immediately before the filing of the action. (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

## Art. 63 of the Tenancy Law: deadline to vacate after the ruling

Art. 63. Once the eviction action is upheld, the judge will order the issuing of an eviction order, which will contain the period of 30 (thirty) days for voluntary vacating, subject to the following paragraphs. (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

Par. 1. The period will be fifteen days if:

a) more than four months have passed between service of process and the first-instance judgment; or

b) the eviction was ordered on the basis of art. 9 or par. 2 of art. 46. (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

Par. 2. In the case of an educational establishment authorized and supervised by the Public Authority, respecting a minimum period of six months and a maximum of one year, the judge will arrange for the vacating to coincide with the school holiday period.

Par. 3. In the case of hospitals, public offices, official health units, nursing homes, health and educational establishments authorized and supervised by the Public Authority, as well as duly registered religious entities, and the eviction is ordered on the basis of item IV of art. 9 or item II of art. 53, the period will be one year, except where more than one year has passed between service of process and the first-instance judgment, in which case the period will be six months. (Wording given by Law No. 9.256 of January 9, 1996)

Par. 4. The judgment ordering eviction will set the amount of the deposit for the case of provisional enforcement.

## Art. 64 of the Tenancy Law: provisional enforcement of eviction and deposit

Art. 64. Except in actions founded on art. 9, provisional enforcement of the eviction will depend on a deposit of not less than 6 (six) months nor more than 12 (twelve) months of rent, updated to the date the deposit is posted. (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

Par. 1. The deposit can be in rem or by surety and will be posted in the records of the provisional enforcement.

Par. 2. If the judgment or the decision that granted the eviction by injunction is reversed, the amount of the deposit will go to the defendant, as minimum compensation for losses and damages, and the defendant can claim the excess difference in a separate action.

## Art. 65 of the Tenancy Law: carrying out the eviction and protection in case of bereavement

Art. 65. Once the period set for vacating ends, counted from the date of notification, the eviction will be carried out, if necessary with the use of force, including breaking in.

Par. 1. Furniture and utensils will be delivered to the custody of a depositary, if the evicted person does not want to remove them.

Par. 2. The eviction cannot be carried out until the thirtieth day following the death of the spouse, ascendant, descendant or sibling of any of the persons living in the property.

#### Art. 66 of the Tenancy Law: property abandoned during the action

Art. 66. When the property is abandoned after the action has been filed, the landlord can take possession of the property.
