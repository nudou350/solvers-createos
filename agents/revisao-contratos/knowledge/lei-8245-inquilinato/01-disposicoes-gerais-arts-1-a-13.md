# Law 8.245/1991: general provisions (arts. 1 to 13)

> Updated September 2026. This does not replace the advice of a lawyer.

Official text of Law No. 8.245 of October 18, 1991 (Brazilian Tenancy Law, "Lei do Inquilinato"), according to the compilation of the Presidency of the Republic (planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8245.htm), consulted on 2026-09-30. Repealed passages were omitted; the notes "(Wording given by...)" and "(Added by...)" come from the compilation itself. Latest amendments to this law up to the date consulted: Law 12.112/2009 and Law 12.744/2012. The wording below is an unofficial English translation of the official Portuguese text; the Portuguese original is the only authoritative version. Legal texts are not protected by copyright (Law 9.610/1998, art. 8, IV).

## Art. 1 of the Tenancy Law: what the Tenancy Law governs and what is left out

Art. 1. The lease of urban property is governed by the provisions of this law:

Sole paragraph. The following continue to be governed by the Civil Code and by special laws:

a) leases:

1. of property owned by the Union, the States and the Municipalities, and their autarchies and public foundations;

2. of standalone garage spaces or vehicle parking spaces;

3. of spaces intended for advertising;

4. in apart-hotels, residence hotels or equivalent, understood as those that provide regular services to their users and are authorized to operate as such;

b) financial leasing, in any of its forms.

## Art. 2 of the Tenancy Law: several landlords or tenants (joint liability)

Art. 2. When there is more than one landlord or more than one tenant, they are understood to be jointly liable unless otherwise stipulated.

Sole paragraph. The occupants of multi-family collective housing are presumed to be tenants or subtenants.

## Art. 3 of the Tenancy Law: contract term and spouse's consent (10 years or more)

Art. 3. The lease contract can be agreed for any term, depending on the spouse's consent (vênia conjugal) if the term is ten years or more.

Sole paragraph. Without the spouse's consent, the spouse is not obliged to observe the excess term.

## Art. 4 of the Tenancy Law: proportional penalty for early return and waiver for a job transfer

Art. 4. During the term stipulated for the duration of the contract, the landlord cannot take back the rented property. With the exception of what is stipulated in par. 2 of art. 54-A, the tenant, however, can return it, paying the agreed penalty, proportional to the period of the contract already served, or, in its absence, the one set by the court. (Wording given by Law No. 12.744 of 2012)

Sole paragraph. The tenant will be exempt from the penalty if the return of the property results from a transfer, by their employer, private or public, to provide services in a location different from where the contract began, and if they notify the landlord in writing at least thirty days in advance.

## Art. 5 of the Tenancy Law: an eviction action is the only way for the landlord to recover the property

Art. 5. Whatever the grounds for the end of the lease, the landlord's action to recover the property is the eviction action (ação de despejo).

Sole paragraph. The provisions of this article do not apply if the lease ends as a result of expropriation, with the expropriating party taking possession of the property.

## Art. 6 of the Tenancy Law: the tenant leaves an indefinite-term contract with 30 days' notice

Art. 6. The tenant can terminate an indefinite-term lease by written notice to the landlord, at least thirty days in advance.

Sole paragraph. In the absence of the notice, the landlord can demand an amount corresponding to one month of rent and charges, in force at the time of the termination.

## Art. 7 of the Tenancy Law: end of usufruct or fideicommissum

Art. 7. In cases of extinction of usufruct or fideicommissum, the lease entered into by the usufructuary or fiduciary can be terminated, with thirty days to vacate, unless there was written acquiescence from the bare owner or the fideicommissary, or if ownership has been consolidated in the hands of the usufructuary or fiduciary.

Sole paragraph. The termination must be exercised within ninety days from the extinction of the fideicommissum or the registration of the extinction of the usufruct, and after that period agreement to keep the lease is presumed.

## Art. 8 of the Tenancy Law: sale of the rented property and validity clause

Art. 8. If the property is sold during the lease, the buyer can terminate the contract, with ninety days to vacate, unless the lease is for a fixed term and the contract contains a validity clause in case of sale and is registered in the property's title record (matrícula).

Par. 1. The same right belongs to the promissory buyer and the promissory assignee, irrevocably, with possession of the property and a title registered in its title record.

Par. 2. The termination must be exercised within ninety days from the registration of the sale or the promise, and after that period agreement to keep the lease is presumed.

## Art. 9 of the Tenancy Law: when the lease can be undone (agreement, breach, non-payment, urgent repairs)

Art. 9. The lease can also be undone:

I - by mutual agreement;

II - as a result of a legal or contractual breach;

III - as a result of non-payment of rent and other charges;

IV - to carry out urgent repairs ordered by the Public Authority, which cannot normally be carried out with the tenant remaining in the property or which, if they can, the tenant refuses to allow.

#### Art. 10 of the Tenancy Law: death of the landlord

Art. 10. If the landlord dies, the lease passes to the heirs.

## Art. 11 of the Tenancy Law: death of the tenant

Art. 11. If the tenant dies, the following are subrogated to their rights and obligations:

I - in leases for residential purposes, the surviving spouse or partner and, successively, the necessary heirs and the persons who lived in economic dependence on the deceased, provided they live in the property;

II - in leases for non-residential purposes, the estate and, where applicable, their successor in the business.

## Art. 12 of the Tenancy Law: separation, divorce and end of a stable union; release of the guarantor

Art. 12. In cases of de facto separation, judicial separation, divorce or dissolution of a stable union, the residential lease continues automatically with the spouse or partner who remains in the property. (Wording given by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

Par. 1. In the cases provided for in this article and in art. 11, the subrogation will be communicated in writing to the landlord and to the guarantor, if that is the type of lease guarantee. (Added by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

Par. 2. The guarantor can be released from their responsibilities within 30 (thirty) days from receipt of the communication from the subrogated party, remaining liable for the effects of the surety for 120 (one hundred and twenty) days after notification to the landlord. (Added by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)

## Art. 13 of the Tenancy Law: assignment, subletting and lending require written consent

Art. 13. Assignment of the lease, subletting and lending of the property, in whole or in part, depend on the landlord's prior written consent.

Par. 1. Consent is not presumed from the landlord's mere delay in formally stating their opposition.

Par. 2. Once notified in writing by the tenant of the occurrence of one of the cases of this article, the landlord has thirty days to formally state their opposition.

Par. 3. (VETOED) (Added by Law No. 12.112 of 2009)
