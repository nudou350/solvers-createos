# Law 8.245/1991: subletting and rent (arts. 14 to 21)

> Updated September 2026. This does not replace the advice of a lawyer.

Official text of Law No. 8.245 of October 18, 1991 (Brazilian Tenancy Law, "Lei do Inquilinato"), according to the compilation of the Presidency of the Republic (planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8245.htm), consulted on 2026-09-30. Repealed passages were omitted; the notes "(Wording given by...)" and "(Added by...)" come from the compilation itself. Latest amendments to this law up to the date consulted: Law 12.112/2009 and Law 12.744/2012. The wording below is an unofficial English translation of the official Portuguese text; the Portuguese original is the only authoritative version. Legal texts are not protected by copyright (Law 9.610/1998, art. 8, IV).

#### Art. 14 of the Tenancy Law: rules for subleases

Art. 14. The provisions on leases apply to subleases, where applicable.

## Art. 15 of the Tenancy Law: the end of the lease ends the subleases

Art. 15. When the lease is rescinded or ends, whatever the cause, the subleases are terminated, with the subtenant's right to compensation against the sublessor ensured.

## Art. 16 of the Tenancy Law: the subtenant's liability

Art. 16. The subtenant is secondarily liable to the landlord for the amount owed to the sublessor, when the latter is sued, and also for the rent that falls due during the lawsuit.

## Art. 17 of the Tenancy Law: free rent agreement, prohibited in foreign currency, exchange rate or minimum wage

Art. 17. The agreement on the rent is free, but it is prohibited to stipulate it in foreign currency or to tie it to the exchange rate or to the minimum wage.

Sole paragraph. In residential leases, the adjustment criteria provided for in the specific legislation will be observed.

## Art. 18 of the Tenancy Law: agreement on a new amount or a new adjustment clause

Art. 18. The parties are allowed to set, by mutual agreement, a new amount for the rent, as well as to insert or modify an adjustment clause.

## Art. 19 of the Tenancy Law: review action after three years

Art. 19. In the absence of agreement, the landlord or tenant, after three years of the contract or of the agreement previously made, can ask for a court review of the rent, in order to adjust it to the market price.

## Art. 20 of the Tenancy Law: prohibition on charging advance rent

Art. 20. Except in the cases of art. 42 and short-stay rentals, the landlord cannot demand advance payment of rent.

## Art. 21 of the Tenancy Law: rent in a sublease

Art. 21. The rent of the sublease cannot exceed that of the lease; in multi-family collective housing, the sum of the rents cannot be more than double the amount of the lease.

Sole paragraph. Breach of this article authorizes the subtenant to reduce the rent to the limits established in it.
