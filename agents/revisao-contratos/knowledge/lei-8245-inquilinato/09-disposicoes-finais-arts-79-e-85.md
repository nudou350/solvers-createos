#### Art. 79 of the Tenancy Law: application of the Civil Code and the Code of Civil Procedure where the law is silent

Art. 79. Where this law is silent, the rules of the Civil Code and the Code of Civil Procedure apply.

## Art. 85 of the Tenancy Law: freedom of price, frequency and index in residential leases

Art. 85. In residential leases, the agreement on rent is free as to price, frequency and adjustment index, but tying it to changes in the minimum wage, the exchange rate and foreign currency is prohibited:

I of new properties, with an occupancy permit (habite-se) granted from the entry into force of this law;

II - of the other properties not covered by the previous item, in relation to contracts entered into after five years from the entry into force of this law.
