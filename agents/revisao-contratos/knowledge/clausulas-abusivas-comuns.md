# Illegal, abusive or attention clauses in residential rental contracts

> Updated October 2026. General guidance on urban residential leases in Brazil (Law 8.245/1991), not legal advice: it does not replace the advice of a lawyer or the Public Defender's Office (Defensoria Pública).

## How to classify clauses: ok, attention, illegal or abusive

- **ok**: complies with the law and is balanced.
- **attention**: legal, but expensive, vague or unfavorable; worth negotiating or understanding the risk.
- **illegal/abusive**: contradicts an express article of the law (void, art. 45 of Law 8.245/1991) or is a manifestly excessive penalty.

Always cite the article. If the knowledge base does not cover the case, say that you did not find a specific rule and mark it "confirm with a lawyer".

## The most common illegal clauses in residential leases

| Clause | Why it is illegal | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** penalty for early exit, with no proportionality | The penalty must be proportional to the time served | Law 8.245, art. 4 and art. 45 |
| More than one guarantee (guarantor + deposit, rental guarantee insurance + deposit) | More than one type is prohibited, on pain of nullity; misdemeanor | art. 37, sole paragraph; art. 43, II |
| Cash deposit above 3 months' rent | Legal limit of 3 months | art. 38, par. 2 |
| Advance rent with a guarantee (outside short-stay rentals) | Prohibited; misdemeanor | art. 20; art. 43, III |
| Contract, registration or brokerage fee charged to the tenant | Landlord's charges | art. 22, VII; art. 43, I |
| Extraordinary condominium expenses (facade, reserve fund, structural works) passed to the tenant | Landlord's obligation | art. 22, X and sole paragraph |
| Semiannual or monthly rent adjustment | A period shorter than one year is void | Law 10.192/2001, art. 2, par. 1 |
| Rent in dollars or tied to the minimum wage | Prohibited | art. 17; art. 85 |
| Ban on automatic renewal in a contract shorter than 30 months | Void | art. 45; art. 47 |
| Landlord can take the property back "at any time" during the term | The landlord cannot recover the property during the term | art. 4; art. 9 |
| Landlord can change the lock or remove belongings if rent is late | Recovery only through an eviction action | art. 5 |
| Guarantor liable for an amendment they did not sign | Not liable | STJ Precedent 214 |

## Attention clauses (legal, but negotiate)

| Clause | Point of attention | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Termination penalty above 3 months' rent | Can be reduced if excessive | art. 4; Civil Code, art. 413 |
| Late fine above 10% or interest above 1% per month | May be excessive | Civil Code, arts. 406 and 413 |
| Waiver of reimbursement for improvements | Valid, but the tenant loses the reimbursement | art. 35; STJ Precedent 335 |
| IPTU and fire insurance on the tenant | Allowed if express | art. 22, VIII |
| IGP-M index | Legal, but volatile | Law 10.192/2001, art. 2 |
| 30-month contract | Allows termination without cause (denúncia vazia) after the term | art. 46, par. 2 |
| Visits "at any time" | The law requires agreeing on a day and time | art. 23, IX |
| Total ban on subletting or lending the property | Legal: depends on written consent | art. 13 |
| Fresh paint on return | Only if delivered freshly painted and recorded in an inspection | art. 23, III |
| Guarantee lasts until the keys are returned, even if renewed | Rule of the law; the guarantor should know | art. 39; art. 40, X |
| No validity clause in case of sale | The buyer can ask for the property within 90 days | art. 8 |
| Jurisdiction (forum) in a distant city | The action runs in the property's forum, unless a forum is chosen | art. 58, II |

## High-risk signals that call for a lawyer

- There is already a **notice, eviction action** or court collection (arts. 59 and 62 have 15-day deadlines).
- High amounts (high rent, a penalty above several months' rent, a large deposit) or a long contract (10 years requires the spouse's consent, art. 3).
- A property with documentation problems, a landlord who is not the owner on the title record (matrícula), a sale in progress.
- Suspected scam (a request for a deposit before visiting, a price far below market, a "landlord" who shows no documents).
- A guarantor with a single property (risk of seizure of the family home, STJ Precedent 549).
- Non-residential, mixed-use or short-stay property: the analysis changes (arts. 48 to 57).
