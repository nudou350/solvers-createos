# Lease guarantees: security deposit, guarantor, rental guarantee insurance (arts. 37 to 42)

> Updated October 2026. General guidance on urban residential leases in Brazil (Law 8.245/1991), not legal advice: it does not replace the advice of a lawyer or the Public Defender's Office (Defensoria Pública).

## Which guarantees the law allows (art. 37)

The landlord can require **one** of these (Law 8.245/1991, art. 37):
1. **security deposit** (caução: in cash, or in movable or immovable property, art. 38);
2. **surety** (fiança: a guarantor);
3. **rental guarantee insurance** (seguro de fiança locatícia, "seguro-fiança", art. 41);
4. **fiduciary assignment of investment fund shares** (item added by Law 11.196/2005).

Other products sold as a "guarantee" (capitalization bonds, private guarantee companies) are usually classed by the market as a deposit or surety; check carefully what the contract says and how much it costs.

## Only one guarantee per contract (art. 37, sole paragraph)

- **More than one type of guarantee** in the same contract is **prohibited, on pain of nullity** (art. 37, sole paragraph).
- Requiring more than one guarantee is a **criminal misdemeanor** (art. 43, II), punishable by simple imprisonment of 5 days to 6 months or a fine of 3 to 12 months' rent paid to the tenant.
- Prohibited examples: a guarantor **and** a deposit; rental guarantee insurance **and** a 2-month deposit; two kinds of deposit called by different names.
- Two guarantors in the same contract are **not** two types: it is a single type (surety) with more than one guarantor. That is allowed.
- Practical consequence: the extra guarantee is void and the tenant can ask for what was given in excess back. If unsure which guarantee falls, ask for professional help.

## Cash deposit: 3-month limit (art. 38)

- A cash deposit **cannot exceed 3 months' rent** (art. 38, par. 2).
- It must be **placed in a savings account**, and **the earnings belong to the tenant** when it is returned (art. 38, par. 2).
- A deposit in movable property must be registered at a registry of deeds and documents (cartório de títulos e documentos); in immovable property, it must be registered in the property's title record (matrícula) (art. 38, par. 1).
- On move-out, the deposit is usually used to offset proven debts (rent, bills, repairs noted in the inspection). The balance, with earnings, goes back to the tenant. Ask the contract to state the deadline for returning it.

## Guarantor: responsibilities and release (arts. 39 and 40)

- Unless a clause says otherwise, the guarantee lasts **until the keys are actually returned**, even if the contract is extended for an indefinite term (art. 39).
- When the contract is extended for an indefinite term, the guarantor can **be released** by notifying the landlord, but remains liable for **120 days** after the notice (art. 40, X).
- In case of separation, divorce or death of the tenant, the guarantor can be released within 30 days of being informed, remaining liable for 120 days (art. 12, par. 2).
- Cases in which the landlord can demand **a new guarantor or another guarantee**: death, bankruptcy or insolvency of the guarantor, sale of all their properties, release, among others (art. 40, I to IX). The tenant has **30 days** to provide a new guarantee after being notified, on pain of termination of the lease (art. 40, sole paragraph).
- The guarantor is **not liable for amendments** they did not sign (STJ Precedent 214).
- **The guarantor's family home can be seized** for rent debt (Law 8.009/1990, art. 3, VII, added by art. 82 of Law 8.245; STJ Precedent 549; the STF set in Theme 1127 (RE 1307334, 2022) that seizing the family home of a lease guarantor, residential or commercial, is constitutional). Explain this to the guarantor before they sign.

## Rental guarantee insurance (art. 41)

- It covers **all of the tenant's obligations** (art. 41), according to the policy.
- The insurance premium is paid by the **tenant** (art. 23, XI).
- Compare: annual cost (usually a fraction of the annual rent), deductibles, whether it covers damage to the property and the penalty, and whether the premium is renewed each year.
- Rental guarantee insurance **plus** a deposit or guarantor is two guarantees (art. 37, sole paragraph).
- Do not confuse it with **fire insurance**, which is a different insurance and is **not a lease guarantee**; by law it is the landlord's, unless an express clause says otherwise (art. 22, VIII).

## Contract with no guarantee (art. 42) and injunction eviction

- With no guarantee at all, the landlord can demand the rent **up to the 6th business day of the month that is starting** (the upcoming month), that is, in advance (art. 42).
- With no guarantee, non-payment allows an **eviction injunction in 15 days** (art. 59, par. 1, IX), which the tenant can avoid by depositing everything owed within the deadline (art. 59, par. 3).
