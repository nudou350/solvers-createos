I don't have live market data, so here is the latest figure I have, dated: the **Selic target is 13.75% a year**, set by the Copom (the Central Bank's monetary policy committee) on **September 16, 2026**, a cut of 0.25 point from 14.00%. It was the fifth cut in a row since March 2026 (15.00% in January, then 14.75%, 14.50%, 14.25%, 14.00% and 13.75%). The next meetings are November 3 and 4, and December 8 and 9, 2026. The Selic changes at every meeting (8 a year), so please check bcb.gov.br for the current value.

The CDI, the rate on overnight loans between banks, tracks the Selic about 0.10 point below, so it is near 13.65% a year (reference of September 25, 2026).

**What it changes for poupança (Lei 12.703/2012)**

| Selic | Poupança yield |
|---|---|
| Above 8.5% a year | 0.5% a month + TR |
| Equal to or below 8.5% a year | 70% of the Selic + TR |

- With the Selic at 13.75%, the first row applies: 0.5% a month + TR. The TR (the reference rate) is around 0.17% a month, so poupança yields about **0.66% to 0.67% a month, close to 8.2% a year**.
- The Selic falling from 14.00% to 13.75% **didn't change** poupança's rule: as long as the Selic stays above 8.5%, the formula is the same, and only the TR moves the number a little.
- If the Selic ever falls to 8.5% or below, the rule changes to 70% of the Selic + TR.
- Poupança is exempt from income tax for individuals, has no IOF and is covered by the FGC. The yield is credited on the deposit's "anniversary".

**Where that leaves it:** with the Selic above 8.5%, a floating-rate investment with daily liquidity near 100% of the CDI yields about 10.6% a year net (within 180 days, 22.5% income tax) and about 11.6% after 2 years, against about 8.2% for poupança. That is the math, not a product recommendation.

Two questions, if you want to apply this:

1. Is this money your emergency fund or a goal with a date?
2. Do you have any revolving card credit or overdraft? Those cost far more than any savings yield (R$ 2,000 in poupança earns about R$ 13 a month, while R$ 2,000 in revolving credit costs about R$ 300).
