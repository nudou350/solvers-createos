Straight answer: the Desenrola Famílias track has ended, so you can't join it with a discount now. It ended on **August 31, 2026**, with no new extension announced (situation checked on September 30, 2026). From September 1, negotiations went back to normal market conditions. Please confirm at gov.br and with your bank, since these programs change rules and deadlines often.

**Why the other open program doesn't fit you**

The only one still open is Desenrola Adimplentes (until October 26, 2026), and it is only for informal workers with a personal loan that is up to date or up to 90 days overdue. Credit cards and overdraft are left out, so it doesn't apply to an old card debt.

**What you can do now**

1. **Negotiate directly with the creditor.** An old debt often gets a large discount when paid up front. Ask for an up-front offer and an installment offer.
2. **Check Serasa Limpa Nome**, a free private platform with offers from partner creditors, open all year. The larger-discount fair (feirão) happens in periodic editions: the last one I have on record ran from February 23 to April 1, 2026, so check serasa.com.br to see if one is open now.
3. **Decide how much fits the budget before calling.** Never accept an installment that doesn't fit just to "clean your name".
4. **Compare by total amount and CET**, not just by the installment, and demand everything in writing (the agreement and the payment slip). Keep the receipt.
5. After payment, the creditor has 5 business days to request removal of your name from the delinquent-debtor registries (Súmula 548 of the STJ): check afterward.
6. A negative record leaves the registries after 5 years, but the debt can still be collected by other means, so don't plan on waiting for it to "expire".

Questions to ask the creditor: the total to pay off today, the number and amount of installments with the CET, whether there is a discount on interest and penalties for paying up front, and how long until your name comes off the registries.

**Watch out for scams using the program's name:** never pay a "fee to join Desenrola" or a "release fee". Use only official channels. The payment slip must be in the name of the creditor or of the collection company named in the agreement. If the installments don't fit and you have several creditors, over-indebtedness (Procon, Defensoria or CEJUSC) is an option.

Three questions so I can help you prepare the negotiation:

1. What is the current balance, with which creditor, and is your name in a registry?
2. How much can you pay up front, if any, and how much per month without running short of the basics?
3. Do you have other debts besides this card?
