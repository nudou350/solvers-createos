There is a program that seems to match your profile: **Desenrola Adimplentes**, open **until October 26, 2026** (extended by MP 1.393/2026, published on September 25, 2026). That is less than three weeks from today, so it is worth moving soon. I can't promise you will be approved: the credit analysis belongs to the bank.

**What the program does**

It swaps a personal loan for a new operation with interest of up to **1.99% a month**.

**Conditions as reported (confirm at gov.br and with the bank)**

- Only for informal workers: no CLT contract, and not a civil servant, retiree or pensioner. Driving for an app without a formal contract seems to fit.
- A personal loan without payroll deduction, with a balance of up to R$ 15,000 (yours is R$ 9,000).
- Current or up to 90 days overdue, and at least 4 installments already paid.
- Left out: credit cards, overdraft, rural credit and secured financing.
- The analysis is done by the participating institutions (reports mention Banco do Brasil and Caixa): you need to go to the bank before October 26, 2026.
- A provisional measure (MP) has to be voted on by Congress, so the rule may change.

**How to decide if the swap is good for you**

- Compare the CET (total effective cost) of your current loan with the new offer, not just the installment. The swap only pays off if the new CET is lower and the installment fits your budget.
- Ask for everything in writing: the new amount, number of installments, rate and CET.
- If it doesn't work out, credit portability (moving the loan to another institution at a lower rate) is another route to compare.
- Beware of scams: joining the program is free, through the official channels of the banks and the government. Never pay a "fee to join" or a "release fee", and never share passwords.

Three questions:

1. What is the monthly rate or CET of your current loan, and how many installments have you already paid?
2. Which bank holds the loan?
3. What do you net per month on average, and in your worst month, so I can check whether the installment fits?
