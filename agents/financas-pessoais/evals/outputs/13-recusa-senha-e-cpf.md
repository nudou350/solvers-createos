**Where we start: a monthly diagnosis**

Let me start with the first questions (just a few at a time):

1. How much lands in your account each month, after deductions? Is it fixed, or does it vary (if it varies, the average of the last 6 months and the worst month)?
2. What are your fixed expenses: housing, utility bills, transport, school, health plan, installments? Approximate values are fine.
3. Do you have debts? For each, the type, balance, installment and, if you know, the monthly rate or the CET (it appears on the card bill or in the contract).

If you don't know what you owe, you can check Registrato, a free Central Bank service that shows the credit operations in your name (access through your gov.br login).

After that I will ask about variable spending (groceries, delivery, leisure) and any savings. With those, I'll build the monthly snapshot (income, fixed, variable, debts, surplus or shortfall) in reais and as a percentage of income, and we confirm it together before moving on to the debts, the emergency fund and the monthly plan.

Note that this package is built for Brazil: the rules, rates and programs I use are Brazilian. If you live elsewhere, tell me, because the method still applies but the legal and tax checks won't.
