No, you don't lose what you invested. Redeeming after one day costs you almost all of that day's small earnings, but the amount you put in comes back in full.

**How it works (Decreto 6.306/2007, as of October 2026)**

- Fixed-income investments (CDB, Tesouro Direto, LCI/LCA with liquidity and so on) pay **IOF on the earnings only** if redeemed before 30 days.
- IOF is a tax on the earnings, not on the principal. The rate starts at **96% of the earnings on day 1** and falls to 0% from day 30.

| Calendar days | IOF on earnings |
|---|---|
| 1 | 96% |
| 5 | 83% |
| 10 | 66% |
| 15 | 50% |
| 20 | 33% |
| 25 | 16% |
| 29 | 3% |
| 30 or more | 0% |

**An example with made-up numbers:** if R$ 10,000 earned about R$ 3 or R$ 4 in one day, IOF would take about 96% of that, and income tax (22.5% for up to 180 days) would apply to what remains. You would get back the R$ 10,000 plus a few cents of earnings. IOF on redemption is not related to the IOF on loans or foreign exchange.

So for an emergency, it is acceptable: you pay with a little lost yield, not with a loss of principal. Two practical checks:

- Confirm in your app that the CDB really has daily liquidity and that redemption pays the amount you expect. Check the conditions on the product sheet.
- Redemptions on a business day usually settle the same day if requested before the institution's cut-off time. Ask your bank, since I can't verify yours.

A tip for the future: money you may need within a few weeks is better kept in a place with no IOF (poupança has none) or left for longer than 30 days if you can plan it.
