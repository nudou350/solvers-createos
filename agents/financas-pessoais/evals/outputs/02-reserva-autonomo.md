Because a ride-hailing driver's income varies from week to week, the emergency fund should be bigger than the usual 3 to 6 months. It is measured in months of essential costs, not months of income.

**Target fund (variable income: 6 to 12 months of essentials)**

| Months | Fund with essentials of R$ 2,800 |
|---|---|
| 6 (minimum for you) | R$ 16,800 |
| 9 | R$ 25,200 |
| 12 (top of the range) | R$ 33,600 |

If anyone depends only on your money (children, parents, partner), or you have health costs at home, aim for the top of the range. If you live alone with no dependents, start by aiming at 6 months and move up later.

**Timeline**, using a made-up example: if you set aside R$ 700 a month, R$ 16,800 takes 24 months (R$ 16,800 / R$ 700). Milestones that keep it motivating:

- 1 month of essentials: R$ 2,800
- 3 months: R$ 8,400
- Target: R$ 16,800 or more

With variable income, set the monthly amount by your worst month, and in good weeks put a share straight into the fund.

One catch: if you have expensive debt (revolving card credit or overdraft), build only a minimum reserve first (R$ 500 to R$ 1,000, or half a month of essentials, here about R$ 1,400), pay the debt off and then complete the fund.

**Where to keep it (categories, not products)**

The criteria, in this order: safety, liquidity (same-day or next-day redemption) and yield. Categories that usually fit:

- Floating-rate government bond (Tesouro Selic): federal government risk, redemption on a business day. It pays no custody fee up to R$ 10,000.
- Floating-rate CDB or RDB with daily liquidity, near 100% of the CDI, at an institution covered by the FGC (R$ 250,000 per person and institution or conglomerate).
- Remunerated account that yields a percentage of the CDI.
- Poupança: yields less today, but it is simple and exempt from income tax.

For reference, with the Selic at 13.75% (Copom, September 16, 2026), poupança yields about 8.2% a year, exempt. A floating-rate investment at about 100% of the CDI yields about 10.6% a year net if redeemed within 180 days (22.5% income tax on earnings), and about 11.6% after 2 years (15%). Not suitable for this fund: stocks, crypto, long fixed-rate bonds, LCI/LCA with a lock-up period and private pension plans.

To fine-tune the plan, I need up to three answers:

1. How much do you have saved today and where? Can you withdraw it the same day?
2. Do you have any debts, especially card revolving credit or overdraft?
3. Does anyone depend only on you? And what was your worst month of income in the last 6?

Rates and limits change: confirm them at bcb.gov.br, fgc.org.br and tesourodireto.com.br (numbers as of October 2026).
