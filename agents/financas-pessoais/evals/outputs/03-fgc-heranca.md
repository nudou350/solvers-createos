Short answer: putting all R$ 400,000 in a CDB at a single bank is not fully covered. Here is why.

**What the FGC covers (limits as of October 2026)**

- R$ 250,000 per person (CPF), per institution or conglomerate, counting principal and earnings.
- An overall ceiling of R$ 1 million per person every 4 years, counted from the first guarantee payment.
- Institutions in the same conglomerate count as one, so two brands owned by the same group share a single R$ 250,000 limit.
- In a joint account, the limit is divided among the holders.

With R$ 400,000 in one institution, about R$ 150,000 would stand outside the guarantee, and since earnings count toward the limit, the uncovered part grows over time. The guarantee also doesn't make a bank "safe": it pays through official channels, with no intermediary and no fee, but the payment can take weeks. And be suspicious of anyone offering to "advance the guarantee".

**What this means in practice**

- Anyone with more than R$ 250,000 should spread it across different conglomerates, keeping each one comfortably below the limit (leave room for earnings).
- A rate far above the market (for example, a CDB paying 130% of the CDI) usually signals a riskier institution, so a high rate is a reason for more caution, not less.
- Tesouro Direto bonds don't depend on the FGC: the guarantee is the National Treasury. That is a different type of risk, not a better or worse one.
- Taxes: a CDB follows the regressive income tax table on earnings (22.5% up to 180 days, 20% up to 360, 17.5% up to 720 and 15% beyond), plus IOF if redeemed before 30 days.

An inheritance of this size is a big decision. I don't build portfolios or recommend where to put it: for that, talk to a certified, independent professional.

What I can help with is the foundation. Three questions:

1. Do you have any expensive debt (revolving card credit, overdraft, personal loans)? If so, paying it off is usually the best use of part of this money.
2. Do you have an emergency fund today? What are your monthly essential costs? Part of this money could become the fund, which is measured in months of costs and needs same-day liquidity.
3. Is the rest for a goal with a date (a home, a business) or for the long term?

Limits change: confirm them at fgc.org.br and bcb.gov.br before deciding.
