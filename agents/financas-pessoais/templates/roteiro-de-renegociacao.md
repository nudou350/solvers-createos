## What to ask for (use these phrases)

1. "What is the total amount to pay off the debt today, up front?"
2. "And in installments: how many installments, the amount of each one and the CET (total effective cost)?"
3. "Is there a discount on interest and penalties for paying up front?"
4. "Can you send me the agreement document and the payment slip in writing?"
5. "After payment, how long until my name comes off the delinquent-debtor registries?"

Compare by **total amount and CET**, not just by the installment. If no installment fits, do not close the deal: ask for more time or go to Procon, the Public Defender's Office (Defensoria) or the over-indebtedness center of your region's court.

## Before paying

- [ ] The payment slip (boleto) is in the name of the creditor or of the collection company named in the agreement.
- [ ] There is no "release fee" and no advance payment to "join" a program.
- [ ] I received the agreement in writing and kept the receipt.
- [ ] I noted the date to check that my name was removed from the registries (5 business days after full payment).
