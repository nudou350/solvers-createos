# Average card interest rates in Brazil (August 2026)

Financial education, not an investment recommendation. Figures published by the Central Bank of Brazil on September 29, 2026 with data from August 2026. The next release comes out near the end of October: after that, these numbers are out of date. Check the latest value on the Central Bank's website.

## Average credit card rates

| Type (individuals) | Average annual rate | Monthly equivalent (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Revolving card credit (rotativo) | **444.9%** (July: 436.2%) | about 15% |
| Card bill installment plan (parcelamento) | **192.1%** (July: 189.3%) | about 9.3% |
| Card, revolving and installment together | 97.8% (July: 93.1%) | about 5.9% |

The Central Bank converts the monthly rate into an annual rate by extrapolation. In practice almost nobody stays in revolving credit for a whole year, so the annual number is scarier than a single month's math. For the user, the useful calculation is monthly: **balance × monthly rate = interest for the month**.

## What is not in this table

Overdraft has a cap of 8% a month by regulation (see the card and overdraft rules file), not an "average". For personal loans, payroll-deducted loans and financing, the average rates for August 2026 were not checked for this knowledge base: use the average for that type of credit in the Central Bank's ranking of rates by institution or, better, the rate and CET in the user's own contract.

## How to use these numbers with the user

- Averages hide large differences between banks. Ask for the rate on the **bill or in the contract**: the bill must show interest and CET.
- If the user does not know the rate, use the average in this table and mark it **"estimated"** in the debt table.
- Compare options by the **CET**, which includes fees, insurance and IOF.
- Annual rate from the monthly one: (1 + monthly rate) raised to the 12th power, minus 1. Example: 15% a month gives about 435% a year; 8% a month gives about 152% a year.
