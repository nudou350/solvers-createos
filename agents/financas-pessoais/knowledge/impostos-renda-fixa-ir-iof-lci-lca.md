# Taxes on fixed income in Brazil: income tax, IOF and LCI/LCA

Financial education, not tax advice or an investment recommendation. These are Brazilian rules as of October 2026 (Imposto de Renda, the federal income tax, and IOF, the tax on financial operations). Taxes on investments were the target of proposals in 2025 and 2026: always say the rule may change and ask the user to check with the Receita Federal (gov.br/receitafederal) and planalto.gov.br.

## Regressive income tax table

Applies to CDB, RDB, Tesouro Direto, LC, ordinary debentures and fixed-income funds without a special come-cotas (Lei 11.033/2004). The tax applies **only to the earnings**, never to the amount invested, and is withheld at the source on redemption.

| Investment term | Income tax rate on earnings |
|---|---|
| Up to 180 days | **22.5%** |
| 181 to 360 days | **20%** |
| 361 to 720 days | **17.5%** |
| Over 720 days | **15%** |

Example: R$ 10,000 invested for about 8 months (240 days) earns R$ 800. The income tax is 20% of R$ 800, or **R$ 160**. The person receives R$ 10,640. The term counts **per investment**: in an emergency fund with monthly deposits, each deposit has its own clock.

## Investments exempt from income tax for individuals

- **Poupança** (savings account): exempt.
- **LCI and LCA** (real estate and agribusiness credit notes): exempt.
- **CRI, CRA and incentivized debentures**: exempt, but they are more complex products and do not suit an emergency fund.

When comparing an exempt product with a taxed one, compare **net with net**. A CDB at 100% of the CDI redeemed in under 180 days yields 77.5% of the CDI net (100% × (1 − 22.5%)); an LCI at 90% of the CDI yields 90% of the CDI net. Exempt does not mean better, and LCI/LCA usually have a lock-up period.

## Are LCI and LCA still exempt? The case of MP 1.303/2025

In June 2025 the government issued **Medida Provisória 1.303/2025**, which provided for **5%** income tax on earnings from LCI, LCA and other currently exempt securities issued from 2026 on, in addition to a single 17.5% rate on fixed income.

**What happened:** the Chamber of Deputies pulled the MP from the agenda on October 8, 2025 and it **lapsed (caducou)** without becoming law. As of October 2026:

- **LCI and LCA remain exempt** for individuals.
- The **regressive table** (22.5% / 20% / 17.5% / 15%) is still in force.
- The government returned to the topic in 2026, but no new law was found for this knowledge base.

If the user heard that "LCI now pays 5%", explain that this was the proposal in MP 1.303, which was not approved. Ask them to check whether a new law appeared after October 2026.

## IOF on redemptions before 30 days

Fixed-income investments (CDB, Tesouro Direto, LCI/LCA with liquidity and so on) pay **IOF on the earnings** if redeemed **before 30 days** (Decreto 6.306/2007). The rate starts at **96%** of the earnings on day 1 and falls to **0% from day 30**.

| Calendar days | IOF on earnings |
|---|---|
| 1 | 96% |
| 5 | 83% |
| 10 | 66% |
| 15 | 50% |
| 20 | 33% |
| 25 | 16% |
| 29 | 3% |
| 30 or more | 0% |

Someone who redeems within a few days loses almost all of the small earnings, but **does not lose the amount invested**. For an emergency fund that is acceptable. Poupança has no IOF. IOF on foreign exchange, purchases abroad and loans is a different thing and changed by decree in 2025: do not confuse it with the redemption IOF.

## How to explain taxes to a layperson

- "The tax is on what the money earned, not on what you put in."
- "The longer the money stays, the lower the tax, down to 15% after 2 years."
- "If you take it out before 30 days, IOF eats part of the earnings, but the amount you invested comes back in full."
- Annual tax return: investments, including exempt ones, go into the income tax return of anyone required to file. The bank sends the income statement (informe de rendimentos). For tax questions, see an accountant.
