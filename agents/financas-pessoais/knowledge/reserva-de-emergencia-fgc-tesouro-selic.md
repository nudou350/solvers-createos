# Emergency fund in Brazil: size, where to keep it, FGC and Tesouro Selic

Financial education, not professional financial advice or an investment recommendation. This specialist explains **categories** of investment and does not name a specific bank, broker, fund or bond. The guarantee and the products below are Brazilian (FGC, Tesouro Direto, CDB, poupança). Limits and fees change: confirm at fgc.org.br, tesourodireto.com.br and b3.com.br.

## Size of the fund in months of cost

The fund is measured in **months of essential costs** (housing, utility bills, groceries, transport, health, installments you cannot stop paying), not in months of salary.

| Income profile | Target fund |
|---|---|
| CLT employee (formal employment contract) with reasonable stability, or civil servant | **3 to 6 months** of essentials |
| Self-employed, MEI, gig driver or delivery worker, commission-based | **6 to 12 months** of essentials |
| Supports the family alone or has fragile health at home | push toward the top of the range |

Calculation: monthly essentials × months. With essentials of R$ 2,800, a self-employed person needs R$ 16,800 (6 months) to R$ 33,600 (12 months). Timeline: target fund ÷ amount saved per month (R$ 16,800 ÷ R$ 700 = 24 months). Milestones: 1 month of essentials, 3 months and then the target.

With expensive debt (revolving credit, overdraft), build only a **minimum reserve** (R$ 500 to R$ 1,000, or half a month of essentials), pay off the debt and then complete the fund.

## Where to keep it: criteria and categories

Three criteria, in this order: **safety, liquidity** (redemption the same day or the next) and **yield**. Categories that usually fit:

- **Floating-rate government bond (Tesouro Selic)**: federal government risk, yields close to the Selic, redemption on a business day.
- **Floating-rate CDB or RDB with daily liquidity**, near 100% of the CDI, at an institution covered by the FGC.
- **Remunerated account** that yields a percentage of the CDI (check whether the balance sits in a product covered by the FGC).
- **Poupança**: yields less when the Selic is above 8.5%, but it is simple, exempt from income tax and has no IOF.

**Not suitable for an emergency fund**: stocks, real estate funds, cryptocurrencies, long-term fixed-rate or inflation-linked (IPCA+) bonds (they can lose money if sold before maturity), LCI/LCA with a lock-up period, private pension plans and consortium plans (consórcio). Keep the fund separate from the everyday account.

## FGC: how much is guaranteed

The **Fundo Garantidor de Créditos** (the Brazilian credit guarantee fund) returns the investor's money if a member institution fails or is put under intervention.

| Rule | Amount (October 2026) |
|---|---|
| Limit per person (CPF, the Brazilian taxpayer ID) **per institution or conglomerate** | **R$ 250,000** (principal and earnings) |
| Overall ceiling per person | **R$ 1 million every 4 years**, counted from the first guarantee payment |
| Covered | checking account, **poupança**, **CDB/RDB**, **LCI**, **LCA**, LC and others |
| Not covered | Tesouro Direto bonds (guaranteed by the National Treasury), stocks, investment funds, pension plans, debentures, CRI and CRA |

Institutions in the same **conglomerate** count as one: anyone with more than R$ 250,000 should split it across different conglomerates. In a joint account, the limit is divided among the holders.

## Cautions with the FGC

- The FGC pays through official channels, **uses no intermediaries and charges no fee**. Be suspicious of anyone offering to "advance the guarantee".
- A rate far above the market (for example, a CDB paying 130% of the CDI) usually signals a riskier institution. The FGC protects up to the limit, but payment can take weeks.
- The guarantee does not make any rate safe: the risk is losing access to the money for a while, and someone with an emergency fund needs it fast.

## Tesouro Selic: custody and how it works

- **B3 custody fee: 0.20% a year** on the value of the bonds, calculated daily from the purchase.
- **Tesouro Selic pays no custody fee up to R$ 10,000** per person; above that, the fee applies only to the excess.
- The accumulated fee is deducted at events: a sale before maturity, maturity or an interest payment.
- The bank or broker may charge its own fee; many charge zero. Check before buying.
- It carries **income tax under the regressive table** and IOF if redeemed before 30 days.
- A redemption on a business day usually settles the same day if requested before the cut-off time (see the Tesouro Direto website).

## Goals beyond the emergency fund

After the full fund, each goal becomes a "little box" with an amount and a deadline: **goal amount ÷ months until the date = how much to save per month**. Goals of up to 2 years (a trip, a phone, a down payment on something) follow the emergency-fund logic: low risk. For long-term goals (retirement, children's education), refer them to a certified professional: this specialist does not build investment portfolios.
