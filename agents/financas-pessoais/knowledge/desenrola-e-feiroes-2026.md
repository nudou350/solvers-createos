# Desenrola and renegotiation fairs in Brazil in 2026

Financial education, not professional advice. These are Brazilian federal government programs, and they change rules and deadlines often; this file is valid only until October 26, 2026. Confirm the conditions at gov.br and with the bank before advising. Situation checked on September 30, 2026.

## What is still open and what has ended

| Program | Status on September 30, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Novo Desenrola Brasil, Famílias (families) track | **Ended on August 31, 2026**, with no new extension announced |
| Desenrola Adimplentes (informal workers only) | **Open until October 26, 2026**, extended by MP 1.393/2026 (published on September 25, 2026) |

If the user had an overdue debt and missed the Desenrola Famílias deadline, say clearly that the program has ended and move on to direct renegotiation with the creditor, fairs and, where relevant, over-indebtedness. A provisional measure (medida provisória, MP) has to be voted on by Congress: the rule may change.

## Desenrola Famílias (ended)

For the record and in case the user mentions it: it served people with income of up to 5 minimum wages and debts overdue between 91 days and 2 years, contracted up to January 31, 2026. It offered discounts of 30% to 90%, interest of up to 1.99% a month on the new operation and up to 48 installments. It ended on August 31, 2026: from September 1, 2026, negotiations went back to normal market conditions.

## Desenrola Adimplentes (open until October 26, 2026)

Swaps a personal loan for a new operation with interest of up to **1.99% a month**. Conditions as reported:

- Only for **informal workers**, with no CLT employment contract and not a civil servant, retiree or pensioner.
- **Personal loan debt without payroll deduction**, with a balance of up to **R$ 15,000**, current or up to 90 days overdue, and at least 4 installments already paid.
- Left out of the program: credit cards, overdraft, rural credit and secured financing.
- The analysis is done by the participating institutions (the reports mention Banco do Brasil and Caixa): the user must go to the bank **before October 26, 2026**.

If the user is informal and has a personal loan that is up to date, suggest asking the bank whether the operation is eligible. Do not promise approval: the credit analysis belongs to the bank.

## Scams using the program's name

- Never pay a "fee to join Desenrola" or a "release fee": joining is through the official channels of the banks and the government, at no cost.
- Use only official websites and apps. The payment slip (boleto) must be in the name of the creditor or of the collection company named in the agreement.
- Be suspicious of any message that promises to "clean your name" for an advance payment or asks for account access credentials.

## Serasa Limpa Nome and renegotiation fairs

- **Serasa Limpa Nome** is a private platform, free for consumers, with offers from partner creditors. It runs all year.
- The fair (feirão), with larger discounts, happens in periodic editions. The 35th edition ran from February 23 to April 1, 2026. Check serasa.com.br to see whether an edition is open now.
- There are similar platforms at other credit bureaus and at the banks themselves.
